Training Visa Subclass 407 Changes in 2026

Quick Answer: Two big training visa subclass 407 changes took effect in 2026. From 11 March 2026, the sponsor and the training nomination must be approved before you lodge. From 12 November 2026, you must be outside Australia when you apply and when the visa is granted. Applications lodged inside Australia are now invalid.

If you planned to apply for a 407 visa from inside Australia, these changes matter a lot. A wrong step can make your application invalid. It can also leave you without a visa.

This guide explains both changes in plain words. It shows who is affected and what to do next.

What Is the Training Visa Subclass 407?

Who the Visa Is For

The Training visa (subclass 407) is a temporary visa for workplace-based training and professional development in Australia. It is for learning skills. It is not a general job visa.

You need an approved sponsor and an approved training nomination. The only exception to the nomination step is when the sponsor is a Commonwealth agency.

How Long Can You Stay?

The visa can last up to two years. The length matches your approved training program.

Is the 407 Training Visa Still Available?

Yes. The 407 is still open. But the rules for applying are tighter in 2026. You must now have your sponsor and nomination approved first. You must also apply from outside Australia.

What Are the Training Visa Subclass 407 Changes?

11 March 2026: Approvals Before You Lodge

Before this date, the sponsorship, nomination and visa application could be lodged together. Approvals only had to be in place before the visa was granted.

From 11 March 2026, you can only lodge a valid application after two things happen. First, your sponsor is approved as a Temporary Activities Sponsor. Second, your training nomination is approved.

12 November 2026: Apply from Outside Australia

The Department of Home Affairs says applicants must be outside Australia when they apply and when the visa is granted. The change took effect on 12 November 2026.

If you apply while in Australia, your application is invalid. ImmiAccount may still let you submit it, but it will not count.

What Happens If Your Application Is Invalid?

Home Affairs warns that an invalid application can affect your visa status. If your visa has already expired, you may not be able to apply for another visa. You also cannot ask for a merits review of an invalidity decision.

Offshore applicants do not receive a Bridging visa linked to their 407 application.

Who Is Affected and What to Do Now

If You Are in Australia Now

You can no longer apply from inside Australia. Home Affairs says you must leave before applying. Plan your steps in this order:

  1. Check when your current visa ends. Leave Australia before it does.
  2. Confirm your sponsor and your nomination are both approved.
  3. Gather your documents and check if you need a health exam.
  4. Lodge your application through ImmiAccount after you leave.

Home Affairs adds a warning. Do not book return travel based on an expected grant date. Processing times vary, and a grant is not guaranteed.

If You Are Overseas

Your path is simpler. Once your sponsor and nomination are approved, lodge your application through ImmiAccount. Then check your account often and answer any request quickly.

While you wait, you may travel to Australia on another valid visa. You must follow that visa's conditions. You must be outside Australia when the 407 is granted.

If You Are a Sponsor

Start early. Your sponsorship and nomination must be approved before the applicant can lodge. So avoid promising a start date until approvals are confirmed.

If You Lodged Before 12 November 2026

Home Affairs says valid applications lodged before 12 November 2026 are assessed under the rules in place when they were lodged. But the 11 March 2026 approval rules still applied to them.

Work Rights, Occupations and PR for the 407

Can You Work Full-Time on a 407 Training Visa?

Not as general work. The visa is for training, so your work is usually limited to what is part of your approved training. Your grant letter and VEVO show your exact conditions.

If you are also an employee, Australian workplace laws still apply. That includes minimum pay. A partner or child on your visa may have their own work limit, so check their conditions too.

What Occupations Are Eligible for a 407 Training Visa?

It depends on your training purpose. For training to improve skills, your occupation must be on an official occupation list. Your training tasks should also match that occupation.

Other purposes, such as training for registration or professional development, work differently. Check the list on the Home Affairs site for your case.

Can a 407 Visa Lead to Permanent Residency?

Not directly. The 407 is a temporary training visa. The skills and experience you gain may help with a later skilled or employer-sponsored visa. That visa has its own rules and is assessed separately.

Common Mistakes with the 407 Changes

  • Applying from inside Australia after 12 November 2026.
  • Lodging before the sponsor and nomination are approved.
  • Waiting until your current visa is about to expire.
  • Booking travel based on a guessed grant date.
  • Assuming a Bridging visa will cover you after an offshore application.
  • Using a guide that was written before the 12 November change.

Training Visa Subclass 407 Changes FAQs

Can I Work Full-Time on a 407 Training Visa?

Not as general work. Your work is usually limited to your approved training. Check your grant letter and VEVO for your exact conditions.

Can a 407 Visa Lead to Permanent Residency?

Not directly. The skills you gain may help with a later skilled or employer-sponsored visa, which is assessed on its own rules.

Is the 407 Training Visa Still Available?

Yes. But since 12 November 2026 you must apply from outside Australia. You also need an approved sponsor and nomination before you lodge.

What Occupations Are Eligible for a 407 Training Visa?

For skills-improvement training, your occupation must be on an official list, and your tasks must match it. Other training purposes follow different rules. Check the Home Affairs list.

Can I Still Apply for a 407 Visa From Inside Australia?

No. Applications lodged inside Australia from 12 November 2026 are invalid, even if ImmiAccount accepts them.

Will I Get a Bridging Visa if I Apply From Outside Australia?

No. Home Affairs says offshore applicants do not receive a linked Bridging visa.

Can I Ask for a Review of an Invalid Application?

No. Home Affairs says an invalidity decision cannot be merits reviewed. This makes it important to get the steps right before you lodge.

Does the 11 March 2026 Change Still Apply?

Yes. Your sponsor and nomination must be approved before you lodge. Commonwealth agency sponsors do not need the nomination step.

What If I Lodged Before 12 November 2026?

Valid applications lodged before that date are assessed under the earlier rules.

In Short

The 407 training visa still exists, but the rules are stricter. Your sponsor and nomination must be approved before you lodge. Since 12 November 2026, you must also be outside Australia when you apply and when it is granted.

Check your visa end date now, and plan your travel before you lodge. If you are in Australia, get advice first, because an invalid application cannot be reviewed.
