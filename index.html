Questions We Hear Often

Tourist Visa & Education Visa FAQs

A few questions that come up in nearly every first consultation.

What is the financial capacity requirement for a student visa in 2026?

As of 2026 the benchmark set by the Department of Home Affairs is AUD 29,710 for the primary applicant, with added amounts for a partner or school age dependants. This figure is reviewed from time to time, so we confirm the current number with you before you lodge. Since 8 January 2026, India has been at Evidence Level 3, so full financial evidence is generally expected with the application.

What is the Genuine Student requirement?

It replaced the Genuine Temporary Entrant test on 23 March 2024. Rather than a separate statement, it now sits inside the visa application itself and looks at your study history, your chosen course, and your circumstances at home.

How long does a tourist visa take to process?

Timelines move with demand and your passport country, though many tourist stream applications are currently being decided within around three to five weeks. Sponsored family stream cases can take longer, since both the applicant and the sponsor are assessed.

Can I study while on a visitor visa?

Only in a limited way, generally short courses of up to three months. Anything beyond that needs a proper student visa, which is where the subclass 500 comes in.

How many hours can I work on a student visa?

Once your course starts, work rights sit at 48 hours per fortnight while classes are running, with no cap during scheduled course breaks.

Do you handle both tourist and education visas from the same office?

Yes. Many clients start with a family visit and come back later with a course question, or the other way round, so we keep both files under one roof.
