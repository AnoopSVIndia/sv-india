What Changed in 2024

From GTE Statement to Genuine Student Test

Before 23 March 2024, applicants wrote a single Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) statement, often as a free-form letter. The Genuine Student (GS) requirement replaced it with a structured set of questions built directly into the visa application, assessed alongside your course, your financial evidence and your study history rather than as a stand-alone document.

Structured, Not Free-Form

The GS requirement is a set of targeted questions inside the application form, not an open-ended personal statement.

Read Alongside Your Evidence

Your GS answers are assessed together with your financial capacity and course choice, so all three need to tell one consistent story.

Applies From 23 March 2024

Applications lodged on or after this date are assessed under the GS requirement rather than the old GTE test.

Still Deeply Personal

Even though it is structured, the answers still need to reflect your specific history and circumstances, not a generic script.

Where Applications Go Wrong

What Assessors Are Actually Looking For

Four areas come up again and again when a Genuine Student response is questioned.

Course Relevance

Whether your chosen course is a believable next step given your prior study and career direction.

Study Gaps

Unexplained gaps between finishing your last qualification and applying to study again.

Generic Answers

Responses that could describe almost any applicant, with no specific detail about your own situation.

Inconsistency With Evidence

GS answers that do not line up with your financial documents, academic transcripts or visa history.

Questions We Hear Often

Genuine Student Statement FAQs

Common questions about the Genuine Student requirement and how we help you answer it.

What is the Genuine Student (GS) requirement?

It replaced the Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) test on 23 March 2024. It is a set of targeted questions built into the visa application itself, covering your study history, chosen course and circumstances at home.

Do I still need to write a GTE statement letter?

No. The GS requirement does not use a separate GTE statement letter; your responses are given directly in the visa application form.

What makes a Genuine Student response weak?

Generic answers that could apply to almost any applicant, unexplained study gaps, and answers that do not match your financial or academic evidence are the most common weaknesses.

Can you write my GS answers for me?

We help you develop answers that reflect your own history and reasoning; the responses need to be genuinely yours, so we guide the drafting rather than writing generic answers on your behalf.

Does my GS response need to match my financial evidence exactly?

It needs to be consistent with it. If your GS answers describe one plan and your financial evidence supports a different one, that inconsistency is exactly what assessors are trained to notice.

How long does GS statement preparation take?

Typically a few sessions over one to two weeks, though this depends on how much of your background and documentation is ready when we start.
