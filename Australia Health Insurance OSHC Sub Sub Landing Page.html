Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC): What It Covers, Who Needs It and How to Choose

OSHC is private health insurance for international students in Australia, and holding adequate cover is a condition of your Student visa. This guide explains what OSHC covers, how long it must last, the waiting periods, the approved providers, family cover and refunds, so you can buy the right policy once.

The Basics

What Is Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)?

A condition of the Student visa, not an optional extra.

Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) is a type of health insurance designed for international students who are studying in Australia on a Student visa. It helps pay for doctor visits, hospital treatment, ambulance transport and some medicines. Most international students are not covered by Medicare, so OSHC is how you pay for medical care while you study.

The minimum cover and the rules for insurers are set by the Australian Government through the OSHC Deed, an agreement between the Department of Health and Aged Care and each approved insurer. That is why every OSHC policy has the same minimum benefits, even though the insurers add extras and service differences on top.

Visa Rules

OSHC and Your Student Visa: Condition 8501

What the Department of Home Affairs expects you to hold.

OSHC visa requirements
RequirementWhat it means for you
Condition 8501Student visas carry condition 8501, which requires you to maintain adequate health insurance while you hold the visa.
Start dateCover must be in place from the day you arrive in Australia. You can buy it before you travel.
End dateThe policy should run to the last date you may be in Australia on the visa, usually the visa end date.
Family membersAccompanying family members must also hold OSHC for their whole stay on a Student visa.
Gaps in coverAvoid gaps. If you move between courses and providers, you must arrange extra cover for any gap, and the visa will not be granted until evidence is given to the Department of Home Affairs.
If you are uninsuredFailing to maintain cover can lead to visa cancellation and unpaid health debts can affect future applications.

Your education provider or agent may arrange OSHC for you, but you are still responsible for making sure it is adequate. Confirm the insurer's name, the policy number and the start and end dates. See our Student Visa (500) guide for the full visa process.

Benefits

What Does OSHC Cover?

The minimum benefits every OSHC policy must provide under the OSHC Deed.

Minimum OSHC benefits under the OSHC Deed (1 July 2025 to 30 June 2028)
ServiceMinimum benefit
Out-of-hospital medical services (for example, GP and specialist visits)85% of the Medicare Benefits Schedule (MBS) fee
In-hospital medical services100% of the MBS fee
Ambulance100% of the charge for medically necessary transport to hospital or in an emergency, by an approved ambulance service
Public hospital100% of charges for shared ward and other listed services, at state-set rates, for patients not eligible for Medicare
Private hospitalContracted amounts, or the minimum benefit set by the rules
PharmaceuticalsAmounts above the PBS general co-payment, up to AUD 50 per item, with a minimum total of AUD 500 a year for a single student and AUD 1,000 a year for other insured groups

These are minimums. Individual insurers can pay more, for example by covering GP visits at a higher percentage than the minimum, so compare policy documents. Because benefits are paid as a percentage of the MBS fee, you may still pay a gap if your doctor charges more than the MBS fee.

Limits

What OSHC Does Not Cover

Standard OSHC is hospital and medical cover, not general treatment cover.

  • General treatment: dental, optical (glasses and contact lenses) and physiotherapy are not part of standard OSHC. Some providers sell extras cover for these, as an add-on or separate policy.
  • Treatment outside Australia: OSHC pays for care in Australia, so you need separate travel insurance for trips home or overseas.
  • Costs above the benefit: you may pay the difference if a provider charges more than the benefit, and medicine costs above the cap can be significant, particularly for ongoing or cancer treatment.
  • Treatment during a waiting period: some claims can be refused until the waiting period has passed (see below).
Waiting Periods

OSHC Waiting Periods Explained

Waiting periods are the main reason a claim can be declined.

Maximum waiting periods under the OSHC Deed
Type of treatmentMaximum waiting period
Emergency treatmentNone
Hospital or hospital-substitute treatment for a pre-existing conditionUp to 12 months
Psychiatric hospital or hospital-substitute care (excluding emergency treatment)Up to 2 months
Pregnancy-related careUp to 12 months for products under two years. Products of two years or more may have none, depending on when premiums changed.
Other listed servicesUp to 2 months

Waiting periods you have already served under a previous continuous OSHC policy are credited to a new one, which is another reason to avoid gaps. Some insurers waive or shorten certain waiting periods, so check each policy. Pregnancy rules in particular should be read before you buy.

Who Is Covered

OSHC for Partners and Children

OSHC is sold for four insured groups.

OSHC insured groups
GroupWho it covers
Student onlyThe overseas student
Student and partnerThe student and their partner
Student and dependent childrenThe student and their dependent children
Student, partner and dependent childrenThe whole family

Each dependant must receive cover at least equal to the student's, and one premium applies to each insured group. Partners and children must be eligible family members for the Student visa. Family members of a student cannot rely on the student's policy after the student's visa ends, so plan any change of visa ahead of time.

Exceptions

Do Any Students Not Need OSHC?

A few exceptions exist, but most students must buy it.

Some students from countries with reciprocal healthcare arrangements may have access to Medicare, for example the UK, Sweden, the Netherlands, Belgium, Slovenia, Italy and New Zealand. The Private Health Insurance Ombudsman notes that this does not replace the OSHC requirement. Separately, the Commonwealth Ombudsman notes that students from Sweden, Norway or Belgium have some exceptions to the OSHC requirement through their national schemes.

Because these rules are detailed and have conditions, do not assume you are exempt. Check with the Department of Home Affairs, or ask us, before you decide not to buy OSHC.

Costs

How Much Does OSHC Cost?

There is no single price, because it depends on you and your visa.

The premium depends on the insurer, the policy, the insured group, your location and, most importantly, how long you need cover. A longer visa means a larger total premium, though some policies of two years or more can have better waiting-period terms. Prices change regularly.

For a rough idea only, one September 2026 comparison of entry-level 12-month single cover from five providers showed prices between roughly AUD 665 and AUD 725, with couple and family cover costing considerably more. Treat that as a guide and get a quote for your exact visa dates, because it is not a government figure.

Check what the premium includes, including extras, waiting periods and any benefit above the minimum, instead of choosing on price alone. A very low premium can mean fewer extras or a longer wait for mental health cover.

Refunds

OSHC Refunds and Changing Policies

The OSHC Deed sets out when a premium can be refunded.

When an OSHC premium may be refunded
SituationRefund under the OSHC Deed
You never arrive in AustraliaFull refund, less a reasonable processing fee
Visa refused at entry or extension refusedFull refund, less a reasonable processing fee
Delayed arrivalPro rata for the delay
Required to leave early for reasons beyond your controlPro rata until the visa expiry
Granted permanent residence or another non-student visaPro rata for the remaining visa period
Not living in Australia for three or more continuous monthsPro rata for the absence
Overlapping cover with another insurerPro rata for the overlap

Insurers can ask for evidence, such as your visa outcome and travel records, before processing a refund. Dependants' premiums are included in refunds. Check your insurer's process, because you will usually need to apply and keep your documents.

03

Arrange the policy and keep the certificate showing the insurer, the policy number and the start and end dates.

If your visa is extended, extend your cover. If your plans change, ask your insurer about refunds early.

Avoid These

Common OSHC Mistakes

Buying Too Short a Policy

Cover must run for the whole visa. A short policy can leave a gap that delays the visa.

Leaving Family Uncovered

Accompanying partners and children need OSHC too.

Choosing on Price Only

Cheap policies can have longer waiting periods or fewer extras.

Expecting Dental and Optical

These are not in standard OSHC. Buy extras if you need them.

Ignoring Waiting Periods

Pre-existing conditions and pregnancy care can have long waits, so read the terms before you buy.

Assuming You Are Exempt

Medicare arrangements do not replace OSHC for most students, so confirm before skipping it.

Common Questions

Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) FAQs

What is OSHC?

Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) is private health insurance for international students in Australia. It helps pay for medical and hospital treatment, ambulance transport and limited pharmaceutical costs, and holding adequate cover is a condition of a Student visa.

Is OSHC compulsory for a Student visa?

Yes. Student visas carry condition 8501, which requires you to maintain adequate health insurance for the whole time you hold the visa. Your accompanying family members must also be covered. Failing to do so can lead to visa cancellation.

How long must my OSHC last?

Cover must be continuous for the whole period of your Student visa and should run to the visa end date. If your visa is extended, you need to extend your cover and pay the additional premium.

Does OSHC cover dental and optical?

Standard OSHC does not cover general treatment such as dental, optical or physiotherapy. Some providers sell extras cover for these services, either as an add-on or as a separate policy.

Is there a waiting period for pre-existing conditions?

Under the OSHC Deed, hospital or hospital-substitute treatment for a pre-existing condition can have a waiting period of up to 12 months, and psychiatric care up to 2 months. Emergency treatment has no waiting period. Waiting periods already served under a previous continuous OSHC policy are credited to a new one.

Which companies provide OSHC?

The Private Health Insurance Ombudsman lists a small number of approved providers: ahm OSHC, Allianz Care Australia, Bupa Australia, CBHS International Health, Medibank and nib OSHC. Policies differ in extras, mental health waiting periods and the level of benefits above the minimum.

Can I get a refund on my OSHC?

The OSHC Deed provides for refunds in set situations, for example if you never arrive, your visa is refused, you leave early for reasons beyond your control or you are granted another visa that ends the need for OSHC. A processing fee can apply in some cases. Ask your insurer about its process and keep your visa and travel evidence.

Do I need OSHC for my partner and children?

Yes, if they accompany you on your Student visa. OSHC is sold for four groups: the student only, student and partner, student and dependent children, and student with partner and dependent children. Dependants must have cover at least equal to the student's.
