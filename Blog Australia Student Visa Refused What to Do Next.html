Australia Student Visa Refused: What to Do Next

Quick Answer: If your Australia student visa is refused, read the refusal letter first. It tells you why, whether you can ask for a review, and how long you have. You can then ask the Administrative Review Tribunal (ART) to review the decision if you have that right, or fix the problems and apply again.

A refused student visa feels like the end of the plan. It is not. Many students get a visa on a later try once they understand the real reason for the refusal.

The pressure is real right now. Home Affairs data reported in the news shows student visa refusals rose from 7.9% in June 2016 to 24.2% in June 2026. The same report says more than 40% of applications from India were refused in 2025-26.

This guide explains what happens after a refusal, why it happens and what to do next. It also covers the new student visa rules that started on 2 October 2026.

What Happens If Your Australia Student Visa Is Refused?

Home Affairs sends a refusal notice to your ImmiAccount. This letter matters more than anything else you read online. It lists the rules you did not meet, the reasons for the decision and any review rights.

What happens next depends on where you are.

If You Are Outside Australia

You cannot travel on that visa application. Review rights are generally limited for people who applied from outside Australia. Your letter will say if you have any.

For most offshore students, the real choice is to fix the problems and lodge a new application.

If You Are Inside Australia

Check your current visa or bridging visa first. Note its end date and its conditions. If your stay is about to end, act fast.

Some onshore applicants can ask the ART to review the refusal. Being in Australia does not give you this right on its own. Your letter must say you can apply.

Do You Get Your Visa Fee Back?

Not usually. Home Affairs has said it does not usually refund the visa application charge, even if an application is refused. Check your own refusal notice and the Home Affairs site for anything that applies to your case.

Why Do Student Visas Get Refused in Australia?

Most refusals come from a few repeat problems. Your letter will show which ones apply to you.

The Genuine Student Requirement

This is the biggest reason. Since 23 March 2024, Home Affairs checks if study is truly your main reason for applying. Your Genuine Student (GS) answers are the proof.

Refusals often follow a vague or copied statement. They also follow a course that does not fit your past study or work, or a long study gap with no clear reason.

Money That Cannot Be Proved

You must show you can pay for tuition, living costs and travel. Large recent deposits, unexplained transfers and weak sponsor papers cause trouble. Keep a clear money trail and explain where the funds come from.

Document and Evidence Problems

Missing, unclear or mismatched documents are another common cause. Dates, names and study history must match across every file. Wrong or misleading details can also trigger Public Interest Criterion (PIC) 4020, even when the mistake was not on purpose.

English, Health and Character

The wrong English test, an expired result or low scores can lead to refusal. So can a missing health exam or police certificate. Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) must also be in place.

Past Visa History

Earlier refusals, cancellations or breaches count. You must declare them. Hiding a past refusal is a serious risk and can harm every later application.

Is Australia Rejecting 40% of Indian Student Visa Applications?

In short, the reported figure is above 40%. News reports say more than 40% of student visa applications from India were refused in 2025-26. It reported more than 51% for Nepal and 5.8% for China.

The overall rate is also high. Home Affairs data cited in those reports puts it at 24.2% in June 2026, a ten-year high.

Home Affairs says it does not assess applications by nationality. It says each case is judged on its own merits. It also says stronger checks have led to higher refusal rates for applications that do not meet the rules.

So what does this mean for you? A high national rate does not decide your case. A clear, honest and well-matched application still has a real chance.

Can You Reapply After an Australia Student Visa Refusal?

Yes, in most cases. A refusal does not automatically stop you from applying again. But a fast second try with the same papers usually gets the same result.

Is There a Waiting Period?

There is no fixed waiting time for a new student visa application. What matters is whether you have fixed the reasons in your refusal letter. Add new proof, a stronger GS statement and clear answers to each concern.

One exception is PIC 4020. If Home Affairs found false or misleading information, you can face a ban on some visas, often for three years. Your letter will say if this applies.

The 2 October 2026 Rule for People in Australia

From 2 October 2026, most temporary visa holders in Australia can no longer lodge a new student visa application from inside Australia. You generally must be outside Australia when you apply and when the visa is granted.

A few exemptions exist. Examples include some course extensions with the same provider, PhD study and school study. Check the current Home Affairs list before you plan any onshore application.

The Section 48 Bar

If you are in Australia and your visa was refused, section 48 of the Migration Act may stop you from lodging most other visa applications while you stay. Lodging a review application alone does not lift this bar.

Check this before you lodge anything onshore. A wrong move can make your new application invalid.

Can You Appeal a Student Visa Refusal?

Sometimes. The right path is a review by the Administrative Review Tribunal (ART). It replaced the old Administrative Appeals Tribunal on 14 October 2024. The ART looks at your case again and can make its own decision.

Who Can Ask for a Review?

Your refusal letter decides this. In general, people who applied while in Australia may have review rights. Many people who applied from outside Australia do not. If your letter says no review is available, a new application is the main option.

How Long Do You Have?

The deadline depends on the decision type, so use the date in your letter. For many onshore visa refusals it is 28 days. The ART cannot extend the time limit, so do not wait.

What Does It Cost?

The ART migration review fee is A$3,727 on the ART fees page. If the decision is set aside or sent back to Home Affairs, you get 50% of the fee back. If paying would cause financial hardship, you can ask for a reduced fee of A$1,863.50.

How Long Does a Review Take?

It can take a long time, often more than a year. Most student visa reviews now rely on written evidence, so your papers and submission must be strong from day one.

If you hold a bridging visa while you wait, check its conditions. Work and study rights may differ from your old student visa.

Review or Reapply?

Choose review if you believe the decision was wrong or you need to stay in Australia while it runs. Choose a new application if you have no review rights or can fix the problems fast. A migration agent can help you weigh both.

Student Visa Refused? Your Step-by-Step Plan

Follow these steps in order. Each one protects your options.

  1. Save the refusal notice. Keep the letter and the decision record from your ImmiAccount.
  2. Note the deadline. Find the review date in the letter and put it in your calendar.
  3. Check your visa status. Onshore, note your bridging visa end date and conditions. Offshore, confirm you cannot use the refused application.
  4. Find every reason. List each rule the officer said you did not meet.
  5. Check your review rights. The letter says if you can go to the ART.
  6. Check section 48 and PIC 4020. These can limit what you may lodge and when.
  7. Pick your path. Choose review, a new application or another plan, such as a different course or country.
  8. Fix the evidence. Rewrite your GS statement, clear up your funds and match every document.
  9. Get advice before lodging. A second refusal can make the next try harder.

For a new application, answer each refusal reason directly. Add proof, not just words. Declare the earlier refusal honestly.

Common Mistakes After a Student Visa Refusal

A few quick moves can cost you months. Avoid these.

  • Reapplying in a rush. The same papers often bring the same answer.
  • Missing the review deadline. The ART cannot extend it.
  • Copying a GS statement. Generic or AI-written text is easy to spot.
  • Hiding the refusal. You must declare it on later applications.
  • Ignoring your visa end date. Staying without a valid visa causes bigger problems.
  • Lodging onshore without checking the rules. The 2 October 2026 changes and section 48 may make it invalid.
  • Trusting a promise. No one can guarantee a visa. Be careful with anyone who does.

Australia Student Visa Refused FAQs

What Happens If Your Australian Student Visa Is Refused?

You get a refusal notice in your ImmiAccount. It gives the reasons and says if you can ask the ART for a review. You then choose to seek review, if allowed, or fix the problems and apply again.

Why Do Student Visas Get Rejected in Australia?

Common reasons are a weak Genuine Student statement, money that cannot be proved and mismatched documents. English, health, character and past visa history also play a part.

Is Australia Rejecting 40% of Indian Student Visa Applications?

News reports said more than 40% of Indian applications were refused in 2025-26. Home Affairs says it does not decide by nationality. Each case is assessed on its own merits.

Can I Reapply for an Australian Visa If It Is Refused?

Usually yes. There is no fixed waiting period for a new student visa application. Fix the reasons first, declare the refusal and check any bar or onshore rule that applies to you.

Do I Have to Tell Home Affairs About My Earlier Refusal?

Yes. You must declare past refusals on later applications. Hiding one can lead to a new refusal and a possible ban under PIC 4020.

Can I Stay in Australia While the ART Reviews My Case?

If you lodged on time and hold a bridging visa, you can generally stay while the review runs. Your bridging visa notice lists its conditions. Missing the deadline can leave you without a valid visa.

Can I Apply for a Student Visa from Inside Australia After a Refusal?

From 2 October 2026, most temporary visa holders must be outside Australia to lodge a student visa application. A few exemptions apply. Section 48 may also limit onshore applications.

Will a Refusal Make My Next Student Visa Harder to Get?

It can, if you repeat the same weak points. A new application with stronger evidence and honest answers gives you a better chance.

Can I Change My Course or Provider Before Reapplying?

Yes, but only if the new choice makes sense. Home Affairs looks at how the course fits your past study, work and goals. Do not switch just to get a visa.

Do I Need a Migration Agent After a Refusal?

It is not required, but advice helps. A registered agent can read your letter, check review rights and spot bars. Always check that the agent is on the MARA register.

In Short

An Australia student visa refusal is a setback, not a final answer. Read your letter, check your deadline and your review rights, and find out exactly why Home Affairs refused you. Then choose between a review and a stronger new application.

Take your time with the next step, but do not miss any deadline. Every case is different, so it helps to get your refusal letter checked before you decide.
