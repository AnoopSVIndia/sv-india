Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL): How It Works and Where It Fits in Your Visa Plan

Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) lets experienced workers have their skills assessed against an Australian qualification, without repeating training they do not need. Done properly, it is a genuine, regulated process. Done badly, it can put your qualification and your visa at risk. This guide explains how RPL works and how to use it safely.

The Basics

What Is Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)?

A way of getting formal credit for skills you already have.

Recognition of Prior Learning is an assessment process in Australia's vocational education and training (VET) system. It recognises skills gained through work, informal learning and earlier study, so you do not have to study things you can already do. A qualified assessor at a Registered Training Organisation (RTO) assesses you individually against the competency standards of a nationally recognised qualification, which sits at a level of the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF).

RPL is not a shortcut around assessment. It is assessment, using evidence you already have. If your evidence fully matches the qualification, the RTO can issue it. If there are gaps, the RTO identifies them and arranges training to close them before it issues anything. The Australian Skills Quality Authority (ASQA), the national VET regulator, expects the RTO to confirm that you have met all requirements before a qualification is issued.

The Process

How RPL Works With a Registered Training Organisation

From first consultation to a qualification with a statement of how you were assessed.

What a genuine RPL assessment usually involves
StageWhat happens
Initial consultationThe RTO explains the process and reviews your work experience and prior study, including your language, literacy and numeracy.
Evidence portfolioYou compile evidence of your skills, such as employment references, position descriptions, work samples, training records and supervisor or client statements.
Self-assessment and interviewYou assess your own skills against the qualification, and the assessor talks through your experience with you.
Practical assessmentIn many cases you demonstrate skills in a workplace or simulated workplace environment.
Gap trainingThe RTO maps your evidence to the qualification and arranges training for any gaps it finds.
OutcomeIf all requirements are met, you receive the qualification and a statement showing how your skills were assessed.

ASQA also expects the RTO to tell you about any third-party arrangements, including who delivers training and assessment and who issues the qualification. If you cannot find out, treat that as a warning sign.

Who It Suits

Who Uses RPL, and Which Qualifications It Covers

RPL works best for people with substantial, provable experience in a field where a qualification is expected.

RPL is most often used in trades, construction, hospitality, community services, business and similar vocational fields. It is typically used for qualifications at Certificate III, Certificate IV, Diploma and Advanced Diploma level. These sit at levels 3, 4, 5 and 6 of the AQF.

RPL tends to suit you if

  • You have years of real work experience in the field and can prove it.
  • You have no Australian qualification, but a skills assessment or employer asks for one.
  • You can provide references, payslips, work samples and evidence of your duties.
  • You are willing to attend an interview or practical assessment, and to do gap training if needed.

Consider another route if

  • You have little or no experience in the occupation.
  • You cannot document your work, or your evidence would need to be created.
  • Your visa plan needs Australian study that RPL cannot provide.
  • The assessing authority does not accept RPL qualifications for your occupation.
Migration

How RPL Fits With Skills Assessments and Skilled Visas

RPL is a qualification route. It is not a visa, and it is not a skills assessment.

An RPL qualification is a separate credential issued by an RTO. A migration skills assessment is done by an assessing authority, for example Trades Recognition Australia for most trade occupations, VETASSESS for many professional occupations, the Australian Computer Society for ICT roles and Engineers Australia for engineering occupations. Many authorities look at your qualification level together with your verified work experience, so an RPL qualification may help you meet a qualification requirement, but whether it is accepted depends on the authority and your occupation.

For points-tested visas such as the subclass 189, 190 and 491, RPL does not award points directly. What it can do is help you obtain the positive skills assessment those visas depend on. Whether and how a qualification counts for any points category depends on the points table and the assessment, so we check this before you spend money.

Stay Safe

RPL Scams and Warning Signs: How to Protect Your Qualification and Visa

ASQA has focused on RPL that lacks genuine assessment, and the consequences for the person holding the qualification can be serious.

Genuine RPL compared with warning signs
Genuine RPLWarning signs
Individual assessment of your own evidence against the qualificationClaims of no study, no exams or no time off work
A consultation, interview and often a practical assessmentA qualification promised in a few days
Gaps identified and addressed with training where neededA 100% guarantee of success
Clear information about who delivers training and who issues the qualificationAssessment outsourced to unregulated third parties, or an unclear issuing provider
An RTO that appears, with the right scope, on training.gov.auClaims of a fast-tracked pathway to skilled migration

The risk to you

A qualification that was issued without genuine assessment can be cancelled, and a cancelled qualification can remove the basis of a skills assessment and any visa that relied on it. If false or misleading documents are given to the Department of Home Affairs, a visa can be refused and a three-year exclusion period can apply under Public Interest Criterion 4020. Only ever use genuine evidence, and never let anyone create or arrange it for you.

You can check an RTO and its scope on training.gov.au, the national register. If you have concerns about an RTO's RPL practices, you can report them to ASQA's VET tip-off line on 1300 644 844 within Australia, or +61 2 5933 2022 from overseas, and you can do so anonymously.

Get Organised

Evidence to Collect Before You Start RPL

The stronger and more genuine your evidence, the smoother the assessment.

  • Employment references showing your duties, hours, dates and employer details.
  • Position descriptions or duty statements for each role.
  • Payslips, tax records or bank statements that back up your employment history.
  • Work samples, photos, plans, logs or reports that show the tasks you have done.
  • Statements from supervisors, clients or co-workers who can confirm your skills.
  • Certificates, licences and training records from earlier study or courses.
  • Identification documents and proof of your name, if it has changed.
  • Your CV or resume, with dates that match the other evidence.

All evidence must be genuine and consistent. Gaps are normal and RTOs can address them with training, but invented or altered evidence is never acceptable.

Costs and Time

How Much Does RPL Cost and How Long Does It Take?

There is no single national price for RPL. Each RTO sets its own assessment fee, which you pay to the RTO directly, and fees can differ by qualification, level and how much gap training you need. Ask every provider for a written quote that shows what is included, who issues the qualification and whether any third party is involved.

Timeframes vary too. A genuine process includes a consultation, collecting evidence, an interview and often a practical assessment, so it is not instant. Any provider who promises a qualification in a few days, or says no assessment is needed, is a warning sign. The skills assessment and visa steps that follow have their own fees and processing times, which we confirm separately.

Other costs to plan for. Skills assessment fees, English tests, health examinations, police certificates and any visa application charge are separate from the RTO's RPL fee.

Common Questions

Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) FAQs

What is Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)?

RPL is a way of having skills you gained through work, informal learning or earlier study assessed against a nationally recognised qualification. A qualified assessor at a Registered Training Organisation (RTO) checks your evidence against the competency standards, and gaps may need extra training before the qualification is issued.

Is RPL legal in Australia?

Yes. RPL is a legitimate part of the vocational education and training system, and RTOs are required to offer it. What is not legal is a qualification issued without genuine, individual assessment of real evidence.

Can RPL give me points for a skilled visa?

Not directly. RPL does not award visa points on its own. A qualification gained through RPL may help you meet an assessing authority's qualification requirement, and that assessment is what the 189, 190 and 491 visas rely on. How a qualification counts towards points depends on the points table and your assessment, so we check it before you commit.

Will an assessing authority accept my RPL qualification?

It depends on the authority and your occupation. Authorities such as Trades Recognition Australia and VETASSESS set their own rules, and many look at your qualification level together with verified work experience. An RPL qualification is not a skills assessment, so check the authority's current rules before you pay an RTO.

How long does RPL take and what does it cost?

Both vary. Each RTO sets its own fees, and there is no single national price. A genuine RPL process involves a consultation, evidence collection, an interview and often a practical assessment, so be wary of anyone promising a qualification in days.

How can I spot an RPL scam?

Be cautious of claims such as no classes, no study or exams, a qualification in seven days, a 100% guarantee, or a fast-tracked pathway to skilled migration. Check that the RTO and its scope are listed on training.gov.au, and report concerns to ASQA's VET tip-off line on 1300 644 844 in Australia or +61 2 5933 2022 from overseas.

Can an RPL qualification be cancelled?

Yes, if it was issued without genuine assessment. ASQA has targeted RPL that lacks proper assessment, and a cancelled qualification can remove the basis of a skills assessment and any visa that relied on it.

What happens if I use false documents in an RPL or visa application?

Providing false or misleading documents to Home Affairs can lead to visa refusal and a three-year exclusion period under Public Interest Criterion 4020. Only ever submit genuine evidence.
