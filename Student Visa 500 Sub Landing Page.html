The Basics

What Is the Student Visa (Subclass 500)?

Since the Simplified Student Visa Framework came into effect, every international student, from an ELICOS learner to a PhD candidate, applies under this one subclass. It allows you to live in Australia for the length of your registered course, work within set limits, and, in most cases, bring eligible family members with you. What differs between applicants is not the visa itself but the strength of the Genuine Student case, the financial evidence, and the course you have been accepted into.

One Subclass, Every Sector

Schools, ELICOS, vocational training, and higher education all sit under the same Subclass 500, so the application principles stay the same across course types.

Tied to Your CoE

Your visa is granted against a specific Confirmation of Enrolment, so the course you are accepted into shapes your whole application.

Assessed as One File

Genuine Student responses, financial capacity, and English proficiency are read together, not ticked off as separate boxes.

Can Include Family

A partner and school-age dependants can generally be included, provided their financial capacity and health cover are shown as well.

Before You Apply

Eligibility for the Student Visa (Subclass 500) Application

Six requirements sit at the centre of every Subclass 500 application. This is what each one actually asks for, and the evidence a case officer expects to see against it.

Genuine Student requirement

Since GTE was replaced, these answers sit inside the visa form itself and are read against your study history, your reasons for leaving a job or a gap year, and your ties at home. They have to agree with the rest of your file.

Current Confirmation of Enrolment

Issued by a CRICOS registered provider for the exact course you intend to study. If you are on a packaged pathway, the CoE for the first course must be in hand before you lodge.

Financial capacity

AUD 29,710 in living costs for 2026, plus one year of tuition and travel. With India at Evidence Level 3 since 8 January 2026, full financial evidence is generally expected upfront. What is assessed is genuine access: bank statements, an education loan sanction letter, or a sponsor’s evidence with their relationship to you documented.

OSHC for your full visa

Cover has to run from the day you arrive to the day your visa ends, which is usually longer than your course. The approved insurers (Allianz Care, Bupa, Medibank, ahm and nib) price the same minimum cover differently, so we compare before you commit.

English language proficiency

IELTS, PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT or another of the Department's approved tests, sat in person at a test centre (online and at-home versions are not accepted), or an exemption based on prior study in English. Note the score your provider asks for is often higher than the visa minimum, and your result should generally be from within two years of lodgement.

Health & character

Health examinations through a panel physician where your country or course requires them, and police clearances where the Department requests them, usually for each country you have lived in for 12 months or more over the last ten years.

Where Applications Go Wrong

The Genuine Student Requirement, Explained

Since 23 March 2024, the Genuine Student (GS) requirement has replaced the Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) test. Rather than one long statement, it is now a set of targeted questions built into the visa application itself.

What It Looks At

Your study history, why this specific course fits your background, your ties to your home country, and your circumstances more broadly.

Why It Trips People Up

Generic, templated answers stand out to assessors precisely because they read the same as thousands of other applications.

How We Help

We ask the questions an assessor would ask first, so your answers are specific to you and consistent with your financial and academic evidence.

Questions We Hear Often

Student Visa (Subclass 500) Application FAQs

A few questions that come up in nearly every first consultation about this application.

What is the Student Visa (Subclass 500) and who is it for?

It is the single visa subclass under Australia's Simplified Student Visa Framework that covers all sectors of study, from ELICOS and schools to vocational courses and universities. It is for anyone accepted into a CRICOS registered course who intends to study full-time in Australia.

What is the financial capacity requirement for a Subclass 500 application in 2026?

A single primary applicant must show access to AUD 29,710 for 12 months of living costs, on top of first-year course fees and travel. A partner adds AUD 10,394 and each dependent child adds AUD 4,449, with AUD 13,502 in annual school costs if a child will attend school in Australia. These figures have applied since 10 May 2024.

What is the Genuine Student requirement?

It replaced the Genuine Temporary Entrant test on 23 March 2024. Rather than a separate statement, it now sits inside the visa application itself and looks at your study history, your chosen course, your ties at home and your broader circumstances.

What conditions come with a granted Student Visa (Subclass 500)?

Common conditions include 8105 (work limitation of 48 hours per fortnight while your course is in session), 8202 (maintain enrolment and satisfactory course progress), 8501 (maintain OSHC for your full visa), 8516 (continue to meet the requirements you were granted the visa under) and 8517 (maintain education for any school-age dependants).

How long does a Subclass 500 application take to be decided?

Processing times vary with application volume, your passport country and how complete your documents are at lodgement. We give you a realistic estimate once your Confirmation of Enrolment, Genuine Student responses and financial evidence are ready, rather than a generic figure.

What is the most common reason Subclass 500 applications are refused?

Inconsistent or thin evidence around the Genuine Student requirement and financial capacity are among the most common issues, followed by gaps between a student's study history and the course they are applying for. Checking these three together before lodgement is what most reduces the risk of refusal.
