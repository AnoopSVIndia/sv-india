Australian Visa Services Handled by Registered Migration Agents

Fifteen services in four groups. Each card opens a page with the detail, so you can see exactly what is involved before you book.

189

Skilled Migration Visas for Permanent Residency in Australia

Points-tested and nominated visas for skilled workers, from independent invitations to state-nominated and regional pathways.

189

Skilled Independent Visa (Subclass 189)

A permanent, points-tested visa with no state or employer sponsor. You lodge an Expression of Interest in SkillSelect, need a positive skills assessment in an eligible occupation, and must reach the points the Department invites in each round.

Know more
190

Skilled Nominated Visa (Subclass 190)

A permanent visa for skilled workers nominated by an Australian state or territory government. A nomination adds 5 points, and it usually comes with a commitment to live and work in that state for an initial period.

Know more
491

Skilled Work Regional Visa (Subclass 491)

A five-year provisional visa for skilled workers who will live and work in a designated regional area. State, territory or eligible family sponsorship adds 15 points, and the visa opens a pathway to permanent residency through the subclass 191.

Know more
191

Permanent Residence Skilled Regional Visa (Subclass 191)

The permanent residency step for eligible holders of a 491 or 494 visa. You generally need three years of regional living and working, compliance with your visa conditions and ATO notices of assessment, so we start preparing your evidence early.

Know more
476

Skilled Recognised Graduate Visa (Subclass 476)

The 476 closed to new applications on 1 July 2024. If you are an engineering graduate or a current 476 holder, we review your qualification, study location and work history and tell you which options are still open, such as the Temporary Graduate visa (485) or a skilled visa.

Our Process

From Eligibility Review to Visa Grant: What Happens at Each Stage

Timing differs between a points-tested skilled visa, a sponsored work visa and a partner visa. These are the stages most cases pass through.

01

Eligibility and Points Review

In a free first consultation we look at your age, qualifications, work history, English level and relationships, and shortlist the visa subclasses worth pursuing.

02

Assessments and Supporting Evidence

You receive a tailored list covering items such as a skills assessment, an IELTS, PTE Academic or other approved English test, police certificates, payslips or relationship evidence, and we review each document as it arrives.

03

Expression of Interest, Nomination or Sponsorship

For skilled visas we lodge your SkillSelect Expression of Interest and handle any state or territory nomination. For work visas we coordinate with the sponsoring employer.

04

Lodgement and Case Management

Once you are invited or approved to apply, we lodge through ImmiAccount, track progress and answer Department requests for further information within the time allowed.

05

After the Decision

We explain your visa conditions and travel and work rights, and plan the next milestone, whether that is a regional commitment, a permanent visa or citizenship eligibility.

Where You Settle Matters

State and Territory Nomination: Choosing Where to Build Your Visa Pathway

For the Skilled Nominated (190) and Skilled Work Regional (491) visas, an Australian state or territory government nominates you, and the place you choose shapes your options. Each government sets its own criteria, which change from year to year.

New South Wales

Home to our Parramatta and Toongabbie offices. Greater Sydney is not regional for the 491, while areas outside it generally are.

Victoria

Home to our Melbourne and Werribee offices. Greater Melbourne is excluded for regional visas; regional Victoria is not.

Queensland

Greater Brisbane is excluded for regional visas. Other Queensland locations are checked by postcode before you commit.

South Australia

Adelaide and the rest of the state are treated as designated regional areas for the 491 and 494 visas.

Western Australia

A separate nomination program with its own occupation list. We check the current WA position before advising you.

Tasmania

The whole state counts as a designated regional area, with nomination criteria set by the Tasmanian Government.

Australian Capital Territory

Canberra is a designated regional area, and the ACT Government runs its own nomination program.

Northern Territory

The Territory is a designated regional area, and the NT Government sets its own nomination criteria.

Regional means postcode, not just city name. The Department of Home Affairs defines designated regional areas by postcode, and a 491 holder must live, work and study in one. We check your exact address before you accept a nomination.

Quick Answers

Australian Visa and Migration Agent FAQs

Straight answers on agents, points and the visas people ask about most. Your own case may need more detail.

What does a registered migration agent do?

A registered migration agent assesses which Australian visa suits your circumstances, prepares and lodges the application, and communicates with the Department of Home Affairs on your behalf. Registered agents must follow a Code of Conduct and keep up to date with migration law.

How do I check that a migration agent is registered?

Search the public Register of Migration Agents on the Office of the Migration Agents Registration Authority (OMARA) website at mara.gov.au. Every registered agent has a Migration Agents Registration Number (MARN). Ours is 2318228.

Do I have to use a migration agent to apply for an Australian visa?

No. You can apply yourself. However, it is unlawful in Australia to give immigration assistance unless you are a registered migration agent, an Australian legal practitioner or an exempt person, so check anyone who offers to help.

What is the difference between subclass 189, 190 and 491 visas?

All three are points-tested skilled visas. The 189 is permanent and needs no sponsor. The 190 is permanent and needs nomination from a state or territory, which adds 5 points. The 491 is a five-year provisional visa for people who live and work in a designated regional area, adds 15 points and can lead to permanent residency through the subclass 191.

How many points do I need for skilled migration?

The minimum pass mark for the 189, 190 and 491 points test is 65 points. Invitations are competitive, so the score that actually receives an invitation can be higher and varies by occupation and invitation round. We assess your points before you spend money on a skills assessment or English test.

Can I still apply for the subclass 476 visa?

No. The Skilled Recognised Graduate visa (subclass 476) closed to new applications on 1 July 2024. Engineering graduates may be able to use the Temporary Graduate visa (485) if they studied in Australia, or another skilled pathway. We review your situation and tell you what is open.

What is the difference between the partner visa 820 and 801?

They are two stages of one application for people in Australia. The 820 is the temporary partner visa and the 801 is the permanent one that follows. Both are based on a genuine and continuing relationship with an Australian citizen, permanent resident or eligible New Zealand citizen.

What is RPL and can it help my visa?

Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) lets an experienced worker gain a nationally recognised qualification through a registered training organisation, without repeating training they already have. For some occupations an Australian qualification can support the skills assessment needed for a skilled visa. It is not a visa itself.

Can you help if I am outside Australia?

Yes. We assist people living in Australia and overseas. Which visa you can apply for, and whether you can lodge from inside or outside Australia, depends on the visa subclass, so we confirm that in your first consultation.

Do you guarantee that my visa will be approved?

No, and no registered migration agent can. Only the Department of Home Affairs decides visa outcomes. What we can do is assess your case honestly, prepare a complete application and tell you early if a pathway looks weak.
