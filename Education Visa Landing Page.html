Before You Apply

Who Is Eligible for an Australia Student Visa (Subclass 500)?

Four things decide whether a subclass 500 application is worth lodging at all. We work through these with you first, before anyone starts gathering documents.

A place you can actually get

Your marks and budget decide which CRICOS courses are realistic. We shortlist before you pay an application fee, not after an offer falls through.

Money you can genuinely show

The benchmark is AUD 29,710 for 2026 plus a year of tuition, and with India at Evidence Level 3 since 8 January 2026 it is usually checked in full. The harder question is whose money it is and how long it has been there.

A study plan that holds together

Why this course, why Australia, why now. If the answer does not match your work history or your finances, that is where applications come apart.

The pass-or-fail checks

English, OSHC, health and character are checkable rather than judgement calls. They rarely decide a case, but they delay one when left late.

Beyond the Student Visa

Post-Study Work Options Once Your Course Finishes

Many international students move to the Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) once their course is complete. Eligibility and length of stay depend on your qualification and study location, so we check this against your specific course rather than assuming it applies.

Post-Vocational Education Work Stream

For trade, diploma and associate degree qualifications linked to the skilled occupation list, generally valid for up to 18 months.

Post-Higher Education Work Stream

For bachelor's, master's and PhD graduates, with maximum stay periods that vary by qualification level.

Second Post-Higher Education Work Stream

A further stream in limited cases for graduates who studied and lived in a regional area, subject to meeting extra criteria.

Questions We Hear Often

Australia Education Visa FAQs

A few questions that come up in nearly every first consultation about the subclass 500 visa.

What is the financial capacity requirement for the Australia student visa in 2026?

The Department of Home Affairs benchmark for a single primary applicant is AUD 29,710, on top of first-year course fees and travel costs. This has applied since 10 May 2024 and is reviewed periodically, so we confirm the current figure before you lodge.

What is the Genuine Student requirement and how is it different from the old GTE test?

The Genuine Student (GS) requirement replaced the Genuine Temporary Entrant test on 23 March 2024. Instead of a single GTE statement, it now sits inside the visa application itself as a set of targeted questions about your study history, chosen course and circumstances at home.

How long does a subclass 500 student visa take to process?

Processing times move with the volume of applications, your passport country and how complete your documents are. We give you a realistic timeframe once we have reviewed your Confirmation of Enrolment and evidence of funds, rather than a generic estimate.

How many hours can I work on a student visa?

Once your course starts, work rights sit at 48 hours per fortnight while classes are running, with no cap during scheduled course breaks.

Can I stay in Australia and work after my course finishes?

Many graduates move to the Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485), which has separate streams for vocational and higher education qualifications with different maximum stay periods. Eligibility depends on your qualification, when you completed it and where you studied, so we check this against your specific course before you rely on it.

Is Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) compulsory?

Yes. OSHC is a visa condition for as long as you hold a subclass 500 visa, and it must cover you for the full length of your student visa, not just your course. We compare providers against what your course and visa actually require.

Can I change my course or education provider after my visa is granted?

Sometimes, but it can affect your visa conditions and your Genuine Student position, so it needs to be checked before you act. We review what a change would mean for your file rather than assuming it is routine.
