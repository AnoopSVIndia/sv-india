Before You Contact Us

What to Have Ready for Your First Consultation

You do not need everything on day one. The more you can bring, the more specific our advice can be.

For Every Enquiry

  • Your passport
  • A current résumé, if you have one
  • Details of your current visa, if you are in Australia
  • Any letters from the Department of Home Affairs

For a Student or Visitor Visa

  • An offer letter or course details, if you have chosen a course
  • Your recent study results
  • Details of your trip and who you are visiting
  • A sense of your budget and funds

For Skilled, Partner or Work Visas

  • Qualifications and work history
  • English test results and any skills assessment
  • Relationship evidence, for partner visas
  • Employer details, for sponsored work visas

Quick Answers

Contact and Consultation FAQs

Straight answers about reaching us, what a first consultation involves and how we work.

Do I need to visit an office to get migration advice?

No. You can book a consultation online and speak to us from anywhere in the world. If you would rather meet in person, choose the office nearest to you from the list above.

What happens in a first consultation?

We look at your situation, shortlist the visas worth pursuing and tell you honestly where your case stands. If you decide to go ahead, you receive a written service agreement with costs set out before any work begins.

Which office should I contact?

Contact the office closest to where you live, since that is usually the most convenient for meetings. If you live in India, start with Chandigarh. If you live in Fiji, start with Suva. If you live in Sydney or Melbourne, choose the Sydney or Melbourne office nearest you. If none is close, any office can help you online.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Bring your passport and a résumé if you have one. If relevant, also bring your qualifications, English test results, any skills assessment, details of your current visa, and any letters from the Department of Home Affairs. For a student visa, an offer letter or enrolment details help. For a visitor visa, details of your trip and who you are visiting help. Do not worry if you do not have everything yet.

Are you a registered migration agency?

Yes. SuccessVisa is a registered migration agency, MARN 2318228. You can check the registration yourself on the public Register of Migration Agents run by the Office of the Migration Agents Registration Authority (OMARA) at mara.gov.au.

Can you guarantee that my visa will be approved?

No, and no registered migration agent can. Only the Department of Home Affairs decides visa outcomes. What we can do is assess your case honestly, prepare a complete application and tell you early if a pathway looks weak.

Can you help if I live in a country where you do not have an office?

Often, yes. We assist people living in Australia and overseas. Whether you can apply from outside Australia depends on the visa subclass, so we confirm that in your first consultation.
