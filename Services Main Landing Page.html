Questions We Hear Often

Australia Visa Services FAQs

Answers to the questions people ask before choosing a service.

What visa services does SuccessVisa offer?

We help with the Australian Student visa (subclass 500), including course counselling, Genuine Student responses and lodgement, the Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485), the Visitor visa (subclass 600) in the Tourist, Sponsored Family and Business Visitor streams, and Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC).

Which visa do I need to study in Australia?

For full-time study in a CRICOS registered course, you generally need the Student visa (subclass 500). You will need a Confirmation of Enrolment, Genuine Student responses, evidence of financial capacity, English evidence where required, and OSHC for your whole visa.

Can I visit Australia on a visitor visa and then apply for a student visa there?

No. Since 1 July 2024, visitor visa holders cannot apply for a student visa while in Australia. If study is your real purpose, you should apply for the student visa from outside Australia.

Is OSHC compulsory for a student visa?

Yes. Maintaining OSHC is a condition of the student visa (condition 8501). It must cover you, and any family members included in your visa, for as long as you hold the visa.

How much are the government visa charges in 2026?

For applications lodged from 1 July 2026, the Student visa charge is AUD 2,500 for the main applicant (AUD 2,050 for ELICOS-only applications), the Temporary Graduate visa is AUD 5,750, and the Visitor visa Tourist stream is AUD 250 from outside Australia or AUD 630 from inside Australia. Charges change from time to time, so we confirm the exact amount before you pay.

Can you guarantee my visa will be granted?

No registered migration agent can guarantee a visa. Decisions are made by the Department of Home Affairs. What we can do is prepare a complete, consistent application and tell you honestly about any weak points before you lodge.
