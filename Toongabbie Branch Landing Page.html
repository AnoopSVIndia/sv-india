About This Office

Migration and Education Office in Toongabbie, Western Sydney

Our Toongabbie office on Aurelia Street gives people in Western Sydney a local place to get face-to-face visa and education advice. Meet a MARA registered migration agent or education counsellor to talk through your goals, check which visa pathway fits and review your documents before anything is lodged with the Department of Home Affairs.

People visit us for skilled migration, partner and family visas, employer-sponsored visas, student visas and university admissions, visitor visas, Professional Year and RPL, and health cover. We explain current Home Affairs charges, likely timeframes and evidence requirements, so you know what to expect before you commit.

Every file is reviewed by a MARA registered migration agent (MARN 2318228) before it is lodged. We explain your options in plain language and never promise an outcome, because Home Affairs makes every decision. Please bring your passport, any letters from Home Affairs and a list of questions.

Common Questions

Toongabbie Migration Agent FAQs

What clients usually ask before they book their first appointment in Toongabbie.

Where is the SuccessVisa Toongabbie office and what are its opening hours?

Our Toongabbie office is at 30 Aurelia St, Toongabbie NSW 2146, in Western Sydney. We are open Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:30 pm, and on Saturdays by appointment only. You can call +61 468 791 726 or email info@successvisa.com.au to book.

Do I need an appointment to meet a migration agent in Toongabbie?

Booking ahead is recommended on weekdays so a migration agent or education counsellor is free to meet you, and it is required on Saturdays. If you cannot visit in person, you can also meet by phone or video.

Is SuccessVisa Toongabbie a registered migration agency?

Yes. SuccessVisa is registered with the Migration Agents Registration Authority under MARN 2318228, and files handled through the Toongabbie office are reviewed by our MARA registered migration agents. Under Australian law, only registered agents, lawyers and certain exempt people can give immigration assistance, so always check the register.

What visas can your Toongabbie migration agents help with?

We help with skilled migration (subclasses 189, 190 and 491), employer-sponsored visas (482, 186 and 494), partner and family visas, student visas (subclass 500) and university admissions, visitor visas, Professional Year and RPL, plus health cover and NAATI services.

Which suburbs do you serve from the Toongabbie office?

Most clients visit from Toongabbie and nearby Western Sydney suburbs such as Pendle Hill, Girraween, Wentworthville, Seven Hills, Winston Hills and Parramatta. If you live further away, you can meet our team by phone or video.

Can you guarantee my Australian visa will be approved?

No registered agent can. The Department of Home Affairs decides every application. What we can do is assess your situation honestly, prepare a complete and accurate file, and respond quickly to any questions the Department asks.
