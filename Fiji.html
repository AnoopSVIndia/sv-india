How to Study in Australia From Fiji

Australia offers universities, TAFE institutes and private colleges with clear pathways from study to work. A Fiji passport holder normally applies for the Student visa (subclass 500) through the Department of Home Affairs once an Australian education provider has offered a place.

Applicants from Fiji often come from the University of the South Pacific (USP), Fiji National University (FNU), or straight from Year 12 and Year 13. Each starting point leads to a different course level, credit transfer and English-test discussion, so we plan the route with you rather than hand you a generic list.

Australian intakes usually start in February or March and again in July. Because the Fiji school year ends in November or December, we suggest you begin four to six months ahead for the February or March intake.

New rules from 2 October 2026: apply from outside Australia, such as from Fiji. Partners and children generally cannot be included, unless an exemption applies, such as for eligible Pacific students (Home Affairs factsheet). Honest note: your course, provider, city and career goals must fit together to meet the Genuine Student requirement. We will tell you if a plan looks weak.

What a Fijian Student Visa Application Usually Needs

  • A valid Fiji passport and a clear record of your study and work history
  • A Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) from a CRICOS-registered provider
  • Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for your stay
  • English evidence such as IELTS or PTE Academic, unless an exemption applies
  • Proof of funds for tuition, living costs and travel, with a clear source of funds
  • Honest answers to the Genuine Student questions, consistent with your documents
  • Fiji police clearance and health examinations with a Home Affairs approved panel physician where required
  • Certified English translations of any documents not in English

Requirements vary by course level, provider and your personal history. This list is a guide, not a substitute for advice on your own case.

What Fijian Applicants Should Know Before They Apply

Practical points for people applying from Fiji. Knowing them early can save weeks.

Match Names Across Every Document

iTaukei and Indo-Fijian names can appear in different forms on a passport, birth certificate and school records. Differences can cause delays, so we check name consistency before lodging and prepare a name-explanation statement when it is needed.

Start Police Clearance Early

Police clearances can take time to arrange, and some visas require certificates from every country you have lived in for a set period. Ask for them as soon as your visa pathway is confirmed.

Show Where Your Money Comes From

Funds may come from savings, family support or other sources. Home Affairs expects a clear, genuine source of funds, with bank records and supporting letters, and cautions against large deposits made just before you apply. We help you organise this honestly.

Translate and Certify Properly

Documents in Fijian or Hindi need accurate English translations. Poor translations and uncertified copies are avoidable reasons for requests for more information.

Permanent Residency and Work Visas for Fijian Citizens

Many Fijians reach permanent residency through one of three routes: skilled migration, employer sponsorship, or a partner visa. The right one depends on your occupation, age, English level and relationships.

Skilled Migration: 189, 190 and 491

These points-tested visas begin with a skills assessment from the relevant authority for your occupation (for example Engineers Australia, ACS, VETASSESS, Trades Recognition Australia or ANMAC). You then submit an Expression of Interest in SkillSelect. The pass mark is 65 points and applicants must generally be under 45 when invited, but invitations are competitive and depend on your occupation and score. Home Affairs has signalled a review of the points test, so we confirm the settings that apply on the day you lodge. Try our PR points calculator for a first estimate.

Subclass 190 needs state or territory nomination. Subclass 491 is for regional Australia and can lead to permanent residency after living and working there for the required period.

Post-Study and Employer Routes

After completing an eligible Australian qualification, many graduates apply for the Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) to gain Australian work experience. The Temporary Graduate visa generally has an age limit of 35 or under, with limited exceptions. Employer-sponsored options include the Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482, with Core Skills and Specialist Skills streams), the Employer Nomination Scheme (186) and the Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional visa (494).

Partner and Family Visas

If your partner is an Australian citizen, permanent resident or eligible New Zealand citizen, you may qualify for a partner visa. Applicants outside Australia usually use subclass 309/100. Applicants already in Australia may use subclass 820/801. These applications turn on the quality and consistency of relationship evidence, not on how long you have been together alone.

No guarantees: we assess your options openly, including when the honest answer is "not yet." We never promise a visa outcome. Only the Department of Home Affairs makes that decision.

Pacific Schemes Open to People in Fiji

Australia and Fiji signed the Vuvale Union treaty on 6 July 2026, building on the 2023 Vuvale Partnership, and more than 100,000 people in Australia have Fijian heritage. The treaty is still going through parliamentary processes, so any new visa settings apply only once Home Affairs implements them. Two Australian Government programs are already open to Fijians.

Pacific Engagement Visa (Subclass 192)

A permanent visa for citizens of eligible Pacific countries and Timor-Leste, including Fiji, with 300 places for Fiji each year. You must register in a Home Affairs ballot (AUD 25) and be selected before you can apply. Main applicants must be aged 18 to 45 when the ballot opens, and need a genuine job offer in Australia. The 2026–27 ballot ran from 1 July to 29 July 2026. Watch the Home Affairs ballot status page for the next opening.

Pacific Australia Labour Mobility (PALM)

An Australian Government scheme that places Pacific workers with approved employers for short-term work of up to nine months or long-term work of up to four years. Fijians take part through Fiji's Ministry of Employment, Productivity and Workplace Relations. We can explain how it compares with other visa options, but we do not recruit for the scheme.

Scam warning: nobody can buy you a place in a government ballot or guarantee a job. Home Affairs warns of scams targeting Pacific Islanders, so deal only with official Australian Government channels. Always confirm an agent is on the OMARA register and use official Home Affairs pages at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au.

Frequently Asked Questions About Australian Visas From Fiji

Do I Need a Migration Agent to Apply for an Australian Visa From Fiji?
You are not legally required to use one, and you can apply directly through the Department of Home Affairs. If you pay someone for immigration assistance, they must be registered with OMARA, with limited exceptions such as Australian legal practitioners. Check the OMARA register before you hand over documents or money.
Is SuccessVisa Registered to Give Australian Immigration Advice?
Yes. Advice is provided by MARA-registered migration agents (MARN 2318228 and 2418515). You can verify any agent on the public register at mara.gov.au.
How Can I Study in Australia From Fiji?
Most Fijian students apply for a Student visa (subclass 500). The usual order is to choose a CRICOS-registered course, receive an offer, accept it and pay the deposit to get your Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE), arrange Overseas Student Health Cover, then lodge the visa from outside Australia with English evidence, financial evidence and your Genuine Student responses.
What Is the Best Visa for Fijian Citizens Who Want to Live in Australia Permanently?
There is no single best visa. Skilled migration (189, 190, 491), employer sponsorship, a partner visa, or the Pacific Engagement visa ballot can each suit different people. The right choice depends on your age, occupation, qualifications, English level, work history and family situation.
Is There a Special Australian Visa for People From Fiji and the Pacific?
Yes. The Pacific Engagement visa (subclass 192) is a permanent visa allocated by ballot to citizens of eligible Pacific countries, including Fiji. Registration costs AUD 25, main applicants must be aged 18 to 45, and you need a genuine job offer in Australia. The 2026–27 ballot closed on 29 July 2026, so watch Home Affairs for the next one. Be cautious of anyone who promises to guarantee a ballot place.
Can I Work While I Study in Australia?
Most Student visa holders can currently work up to 48 hours per fortnight while their course is in session and without a set limit during official course breaks. The rule has changed in recent years, so check your visa conditions on VEVO and confirm the current limit with us.
Can SuccessVisa Guarantee My Visa?
No. Only the Department of Home Affairs decides visa applications, and no registered agent can guarantee an outcome. What we can do is assess your options honestly, prepare a complete application and tell you early if a pathway is unlikely to work.
I Was Refused an Australian Visa Before. Can You Still Help?
Often yes, but it depends on the reason for refusal and whether review rights apply. We read your refusal letter, explain what went wrong and tell you whether a new application, a review or a different visa is more realistic.
Do I Need IELTS or PTE to Study or Migrate From Fiji?
Many applicants need an approved English test such as IELTS or PTE Academic, and tests must be taken in person at a secure test centre. Some education providers accept other evidence for admission, and certain visas have their own English rules. We check the requirement for your exact course and visa.
Can I Bring My Spouse or Children When I Study in Australia?
From 2 October 2026, Student visa applicants generally cannot include a partner or dependent children, and family cannot be added after the visa is granted. Exemptions include PhD students, DFAT or Defence sponsored students, foreign government scholarship holders and eligible students from a Pacific country or an ASEAN member country. We check whether you qualify before you commit to a course.
Can I Switch to a Student Visa After I Arrive in Australia on a Visitor Visa?
Generally no. From 2 October 2026, most temporary visa holders in Australia, including visitor visa holders, can no longer lodge a Student visa application while in Australia. You would need to be outside Australia when you lodge and when the visa is granted, so plan your study visa before you travel.
Will the Vuvale Union Change Visa Rules for Fijians?
Australia and Fiji signed the Vuvale Union on 6 July 2026, and it includes commitments on mobility, education and skills. The treaty is still going through parliamentary approval, and Home Affairs must publish any new visa settings before they apply. Until then, the current visa rules stand.
