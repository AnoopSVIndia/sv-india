Before You Book

Booking & Appointment FAQs

A few questions people usually ask before choosing a branch.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes, your first consultation is free of charge. It's a chance for us to understand your situation and for you to see whether our approach is the right fit before anything is paid for.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Any passport copies, existing visa refusal letters, offer letters, or financial documents you already have are useful, though nothing is mandatory for a first consultation. We'll tell you exactly what's needed once we know your situation.

Can I reschedule my appointment online?

You can reschedule once by contacting the branch directly at least 24 hours before your slot. Please see our Rescheduling & Refund Policy below for the full terms.

What happens if I'm running late?

Please call or message your branch as soon as you know. Depending on the day's schedule we'll either hold your slot briefly or help you rebook, but persistent late arrivals may be treated as a missed appointment.

Do I need to pay before booking a slot?

This depends on the booking option you choose. Booking via website or card can confirm a same-day slot, while bank transfer bookings need 24–48 hours for the payment to reflect before a slot is confirmed. Full details are in the policy below.

Can I book any branch regardless of where I live?

Yes. While each branch serves its local area day-to-day, you're welcome to book whichever branch and consultant suit your schedule best.

Appointment Rescheduling & Refund Policy

At SuccessVisa, we understand that unforeseen circumstances may arise that necessitate the rescheduling of appointments. Our goal is to provide flexible and accommodating service to all our clients while ensuring that we manage our time and resources effectively.

To maintain this balance, we kindly request that clients adhere to the following policy regarding the rescheduling of appointments:

  • Clients are permitted to reschedule an appointment up to one time. Subsequent requests to reschedule the same appointment may not be accommodated.
  • To reschedule an appointment, please contact us at least 24 hours in advance. Not mandatory to get the slot instantly, it depends on availability.
  • In case of any change of mind or cancellation due to any reason, no refunds will be entertained. In case of emergency – valid proof required, otherwise no refund.
  • We appreciate your understanding and cooperation with this policy, which helps us to continue providing high-quality and timely service to all our clients.
Booking Options
  • We have 3 booking options — Via Website, Via Card (any credit or debit card), and Via Bank Transfer.
    • Via Website / Via Card — The client can get the appointment on the same day as per the Registered Migration Agent's availability.
    • Via Bank Transfer — The client must wait for 24–48 hours for the payment to be shown in our account, then only the time slot will be given to the client accordingly. No on-spot appointment available for this option.

For any additional inquiries or special considerations regarding appointments, please reach out to our dedicated support team.
