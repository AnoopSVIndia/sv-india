Business Owner Visa Australia: What’s Open in 2026

Quick Answer: There is no single business owner visa open in 2026. Subclass 890 closed on 22 March 2025. Subclass 188 closed in July 2024. Business owners now use other visas, such as the National Innovation visa (858), state 491 business pathways, or 482 self-sponsorship.

Many guides still talk about the old business owner visa. That can waste your time and money. So this guide shows what has closed and what is open now.

You will also see how much money you may need and whether business owners can get PR.

What Happened to the Business Owner Visa?

Subclass 890 Is Closed

The Business Owner (subclass 890) visa closed to new applications on 22 March 2025. The Department of Home Affairs says so on its own page. It does not name a direct replacement.

The 188 and 132 Visas Are Closed Too

The Business Innovation and Investment (subclass 188) visa stopped taking new applications in July 2024. The Business Talent (subclass 132) visa was discontinued on 1 July 2021.

People who already hold a 188 visa may still move on. For example, Victoria still accepts nominations for the permanent 888 visa and the 188 extension stream.

Why Some Guides Are Out of Date

Some websites still list the 890 visa rules as if you can apply. Some pages we checked were last changed years ago. Always check the date on a page before you rely on it.

National Innovation Visa (Subclass 858)

This visa replaced the old business and investment program on 7 December 2024. It is not based on how much you invest. It is for people with an internationally recognised record of exceptional and outstanding achievement.

You must first lodge an Expression of Interest. Only some people get invited. Reported figures show about 292 invitations from 1,969 EOIs between July and September 2026. Check the official figures before you rely on them.

State Business Owner Pathways (Subclass 491 and 190)

These suit people already in Australia who run a business. A state or territory nominates you. Rules differ by place:

  • Tasmania: You must have run your business there for at least 12 months. It must stay profitable after paying you a set income.
  • Canberra (ACT): You must own at least 51% of a business in the ACT. You must live there for 3 months (491) or 6 months (190).
  • Queensland: The business purchase pathway is closed. Migration Queensland says only the start-up pathway is open, for businesses started between 1 June 2024 and 19 September 2025. ROIs must be lodged before 30 April 2027.

Some agency pages say the Queensland pathway is closed. Use the official Migration Queensland page as your guide.

Skills in Demand Visa (Subclass 482) with Self-Sponsorship

Your own Australian business becomes the sponsor. It then nominates you for a skilled role. The role must be genuine and the pay must meet the rules.

The Department checks these cases closely. Showing a real need for a job separate from your owner duties is often the hardest part.

If You Already Hold a 188 Visa

The 188 is closed to new applicants. But existing holders can still move to the permanent 888 visa if they meet the rules for their stream. Some states also nominate for the 188 extension stream.

Can You Get a Visa to Start a Business in Australia?

Which Visa Lets You Run a Business?

You need a visa that lets you run a business. business.gov.au says this clearly. It also says to speak to a registered migration agent about your application.

There is no stand-alone "start-up" business owner visa today. Most people use one of the options above. Some start the business first and then apply for a state pathway.

Who Is Eligible for a 408 Visa?

The Temporary Activity visa (subclass 408) is not a business owner visa. It is a temporary visa for specific approved activities. Who can apply depends on the activity stream. Most streams need an approved sponsor or endorsement.

One agency notes that the 408 gives full-time work rights. That time can help meet the work or residence rules of a 491 pathway. Check the Home Affairs page for the stream you have in mind.

How Much Money Do You Need for a Business Visa in Australia?

The answer depends on the pathway. Some need no investment at all. Others need a business with set turnover or income. Here is a summary from the pages we checked.

Option

Money to plan for

National Innovation visa (858)

No investment or asset threshold. Government visa charges still apply.

Skills in Demand visa (482)

A salary at or above the Core Skills Income Threshold of A$79,423 (from 1 July 2026) for most roles.

Tasmania (491)

A business that stays profitable after paying you a set income. Two agency pages give A$67,509 for 2026-27.

Canberra (491 and 190)

A profitable business with turnover and taxable income at ACT levels. Sources differ, so check the ACT page.

Queensland start-up (491)

Turnover of at least A$200,000 in the 12 months before your ROI, and a profit.

These figures change. Some update each 1 July. Also plan for government visa charges, a skills assessment, health checks and agent fees. We have not listed visa charges here because they change often.

In the past, investor visas often needed hundreds of thousands of dollars. Those routes are now closed to new applicants.

Can Business Owners Get PR in Australia?

Yes, some routes lead to permanent residence. But the path depends on the visa you choose. Here is how each one works.

Route

How PR can happen

National Innovation visa (858)

It grants permanent residence directly.

State pathway on a 491

After 3 years on the 491 with taxable income on ATO notices, you may apply for the permanent 191 visa.

Canberra pathway on a 190

The 190 is a permanent visa. The ACT requires more time in Canberra and higher income.

482 self-sponsorship

PR usually comes later through the 186 visa, after you meet the time-with-sponsor rule.

188 holders

Eligible holders can apply for the permanent 888 visa.

A few conditions apply along the way. For example, Queensland says you must keep running your business for at least 3 years after the 491 is granted. You also cannot sell or move it without approval.

Always check the rules for your own case. Small gaps in income or ownership can stop a PR application.

How to Choose the Right Business Owner Visa

Questions to Ask Yourself

Start with these:

  • Are you in Australia now, or overseas?
  • Do you already run a business? For how long?
  • Which state or territory is your business in?
  • Do you have an exceptional record in a field such as technology or research?
  • Could your business sponsor you for a genuine, skilled role?

Your answers point to a path. For example, owners already trading in Tasmania or Canberra often look at a state pathway first.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using an old guide for the closed 890 or 188 visas.
  • Trusting one agency page about Queensland instead of the official state page.
  • Missing a state deadline, such as the Queensland ROI date.
  • Starting a business only to meet a visa rule. The Department checks that a business is genuine.
  • Forgetting that income and turnover figures change each year.

Business Owner Visa Australia FAQs

Can I Get a Visa to Start a Business in Australia?

There is no stand-alone start-up visa today. You need a visa that lets you run a business. Options include the National Innovation visa, a state 491 pathway and 482 self-sponsorship.

Can Business Owners Get PR in Australia?

Yes, through some routes. The 858 grants PR directly. A 491 can lead to the 191 after 3 years. A Canberra 190 is already permanent. A 482 usually leads to the 186.

How Much Money Do I Need for a Business Visa in Australia?

It depends on the visa. The 858 has no investment threshold. A 482 needs a salary of at least A$79,423 for most roles. Queensland's start-up pathway needs turnover of at least A$200,000.

Who Is Eligible for a 408 Visa?

The 408 is for specific approved activities, not for business owners. Eligibility depends on the activity stream, and most streams need a sponsor or endorsement. Check the Home Affairs page for the stream you mean.

Is the Business Owner Visa (Subclass 890) Still Open?

No. It closed to new applications on 22 March 2025.

Is the 188 Business Visa Still Open?

No. New 188 applications stopped in July 2024. Existing holders may still move to the 888 visa.

Can I Apply for a Business Visa From Overseas?

Some state pathways suit people already in Australia who run a business. The National Innovation visa starts with an Expression of Interest. Check each visa page.

Do I Need a Skills Assessment?

For the 491 state business pathways, one agency says you need a skills assessment. It does not have to match your business. Check your state page.

Is There an Age Limit?

Sometimes. The 491 usually needs you to be under 45. For the 858, people aged 55 or over must show exceptional benefit to Australia. Confirm the limit for your visa.

In Short

There is no single business owner visa open in 2026. Subclass 890 and subclass 188 are closed. Business owners now choose between the National Innovation visa, state 491 or 190 pathways, and 482 self-sponsorship.

Check the date on every guide, and use official state pages for deadlines. Before you start or buy a business for visa reasons, get advice on your own case.
