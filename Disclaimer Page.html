Site-Wide Policy

Disclaimer & Sources

This page explains how SuccessVisa prepares and reviews the migration information on this site, the official sources that content is checked against, and the general disclaimer that applies to every page — so individual pages don't need to repeat it in full.

Who Reviews This Content

MARA Registered Review

It is Australian Government policy that immigration assistance is only provided by a person registered with the Migration Agents Registration Authority (MARA), or an equivalent exempt person. Content across this site, including branch pages, visa guides and offers, is written and periodically reviewed by SuccessVisa's MARA registered migration agents on that basis.

Not Legal Advice

General Information Only

Nothing on this site is migration legal advice. Visa criteria, fees, processing times and government policy can change at short notice, and the details described on any page may not reflect the most current position by the time you read them. Every page should be treated as a starting point for understanding a visa category or service, not as a substitute for an individual assessment of your own circumstances.

Before relying on any specific figure, date or eligibility criterion, confirm it against the current official source, and where relevant, book a consultation with our team to discuss your case directly.

Where Our Content Comes From

Official Sources We Reference

Pages on this site draw on the following official sources, current as of each page's own last-reviewed date.

  • Department of Home Affairs — official visitor visa (subclass 600) information and current visa pricing tool.
  • Department of Home Affairs — official student visa (subclass 500) information.
  • Migration Regulations 1994 (Commonwealth), Schedule 2, clause 600 — visitor visa criteria, including the sponsorship and security bond provisions that apply to the sponsored family stream.
  • Migration Regulations 1994 (Commonwealth), Schedule 8, condition 8115 — the no-work condition that applies to the business visitor stream.
  • Migration Regulations 1994 (Commonwealth), Schedule 2 and related criteria for all other visa subclasses covered on this site.
  • Department of Home Affairs, global visa processing times tool, for indicative current processing ranges by stream and location.
  • Each branch's own Google Business Profile, for current opening hours, photos and client reviews.
