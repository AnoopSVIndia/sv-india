Australia Business Visa: Business Visitor, the 188/888 Closure and Your Options in 2026

There is no single "business visa" for Australia. A short trip for meetings uses the Business Visitor stream of the Visitor visa (subclass 600). The old Business Innovation and Investment program (188/888) closed to new applicants on 31 July 2024, and the invitation-only National Innovation visa (858) is now the achievement-based route. This guide explains which fits you.

Short Visits

Business Visitor Visa (Subclass 600): What You Can and Cannot Do

The Business Visitor stream is for people who travel to Australia for business purposes and do not work.

Business Visitor stream activities
Generally allowedNot allowed
Making general business or employment enquiriesWorking for, or providing services to, an Australian organisation or person
Investigating, negotiating, signing or reviewing a business contractSelling goods or services directly to the public
Attending a conference, trade fair or seminar, where you are not paid by the organisersReceiving payment for taking part in a conference
Taking part in an official government-to-government visitAny paid employment

The Business Visitor stream is generally granted for short visits, commonly up to three months per stay, and the visa can be granted for a period such as three, six or twelve months. The period depends on your circumstances and on the Department's decision. You must apply from outside Australia and show a genuine intention to visit temporarily, enough funds and ties to your home country. Confirm the conditions on your grant notice, because a breach can lead to cancellation.

What Changed

The Business Innovation and Investment (188/888) Program Has Closed

Many search results still describe the 188 as if it were open. It is not.

The Department of Home Affairs closed the Business Innovation and Investment (Provisional) visa (subclass 188) to new applications on 31 July 2024. State and territory migration websites confirm that new applicants can no longer apply. The program covered the Business Innovation, Investor, Significant Investor and Entrepreneur streams, with nomination by a state or territory government.

The closure does not remove the visa for people who already hold one. Existing 188 holders may still be able to apply for an extension stream where required, and for the permanent Business Innovation and Investment visa (subclass 888), which is available only to people who hold a provisional 188 and meet the requirements of their stream. Stream-specific conditions apply, such as time in Australia, business or investment performance and state nomination, so planning should start well before the 188 expires.

If you are an existing 188 holder, evidence gathering for the 888 usually takes months. Contact us before your visa nears expiry so we can check your evidence against the stream you were granted under.

The Current Route

National Innovation Visa (Subclass 858): The Achievement-Based Option

The 858 replaced the earlier Global Talent visa from December 2024. It is not an investment visa.

National Innovation visa at a glance
FeatureWhat it means
Who it suitsResearchers, founders, innovators and leaders with an internationally recognised record of exceptional and outstanding achievement in an eligible field
How you enterYou submit an Expression of Interest. Only people invited by the Department can lodge a visa application.
NominationGenerally by an Australian individual or organisation with a national reputation in your field
OutcomePermanent residence

The bar is materially higher than the old business program, so many people who would have qualified under the 188 will not qualify here. Check the Department's current requirements and visa charge before you invest in an Expression of Interest. We assess whether your evidence is realistic before you start.

Fit Check

Which Business Visa Route Suits You?

Business Visitor (600) fits if

  • You are visiting for meetings, negotiations or a conference.
  • You will return home afterwards.
  • You will not work or be paid in Australia.

Look at 858 or a skilled route if

  • You have an outstanding international record and a credible nominator (858).
  • You have in-demand skills and an employer or a genuine business (482/186).
  • You meet the points test for a skilled visa (189/190/491).
  • You already hold a 188 and are heading toward the 888.

Costs

Business Visa Cost

Only the Business Visitor charge is given here, because it is the only route most new applicants can use.

Business Visitor (600)
AUD 250

Base charge per applicant from 1 July 2026, for a visa applied for outside Australia.

The National Innovation visa (858) and the 888 have their own, much higher charges. Amounts are set by the Australian Government, normally change each 1 July and should be confirmed on the Department of Home Affairs visa pricing page or in ImmiAccount before you pay. Visa charges are generally not refunded if the application is refused. Health examinations, police certificates, translations and professional fees are separate.

Get Organised

Documents for a Business Visitor Visa

For longer-term business visas, the list depends on the route.

  • Passport valid for the intended stay.
  • A letter from your employer or company explaining the purpose of your trip and your role.
  • An invitation letter from the Australian host, or conference registration details.
  • Evidence of your business background, such as a CV, company registration or qualifications.
  • Bank statements showing enough funds for the stay and return travel.
  • Evidence of ties to your home country, such as employment, family or property.
  • Translations of any documents not in English.
Avoid These

Common Business Visa Mistakes

Relying on outdated advice

Websites that still sell the 188 as open are out of date. The program closed on 31 July 2024.

Working on a visitor visa

The Business Visitor stream does not allow work. Doing paid work can lead to cancellation.

Treating the 858 as an investment visa

It is invitation-only and based on exceptional achievement, not money invested.

Setting up a business that is not genuine

A business created only to sponsor you can fail the sponsor and nomination tests.

Leaving the 188 to the last moment

The 888 needs evidence built over time. Planning late can lose the permanent pathway.

Missing the purpose letter

A vague business purpose or missing invitation is a common reason a visitor application struggles.

Common Questions

Australia Business Visa FAQs

Is there a single Business visa for Australia?

No. 'Business visa' covers several different visas. Short business trips use the Visitor visa (subclass 600) Business Visitor stream. Business owners and investors who wanted permanent residence used the Business Innovation and Investment program (subclass 188 then 888), which closed to new applicants on 31 July 2024. The invitation-only National Innovation visa (subclass 858) is the current achievement-based option.

Can I attend business meetings in Australia on a tourist visa?

The Business Visitor stream of the subclass 600 is designed for activities such as general business enquiries, negotiating or reviewing contracts, and attending conferences or trade fairs. It does not allow you to work for an Australian organisation, sell directly to the public or receive payment for taking part in a conference.

How much does a Business Visitor visa cost?

The visa application charge for the Business Visitor stream of the subclass 600 is AUD 250 per applicant from 1 July 2026. Charges are indexed each 1 July and are generally not refunded if the application is refused, so confirm the amount before you lodge.

Can I still apply for the Business Innovation and Investment visa (188)?

No. The Department of Home Affairs closed the subclass 188 to new applications on 31 July 2024. Existing 188 holders may still be able to apply for an extension stream or the permanent subclass 888 if they meet that visa's requirements.

What is the subclass 888 visa?

The Business Innovation and Investment (Permanent) visa (subclass 888) is for people who already hold a provisional 188 visa and meet the requirements of their stream, such as Business Innovation, Investor, Significant Investor or Entrepreneur. You cannot apply for it without a 188 first.

What is the National Innovation visa (858)?

The National Innovation visa (subclass 858) is a permanent visa for people with an internationally recognised record of exceptional and outstanding achievement. It is invitation-only: you submit an Expression of Interest and, if invited, you apply. It is not an investment visa and the bar is high.

Can I move to Australia to start a business?

There is no longer a general business-owner visa open to new applicants. Depending on your profile, you may be able to use the National Innovation visa, or a skilled or employer-sponsored route such as the 482, where your own genuine Australian business may act as sponsor. The business must be real and meet the sponsor and nomination rules. We assess this case by case.

Does a Business Visitor visa let me stay and work?

No. It is for short visits only and does not allow you to work. Breaching visa conditions can lead to cancellation and affect future applications.
