Who We Are

About SuccessVisa: Registered Migration Agents and Education Consultants

Migration is one of the biggest decisions a family makes. It deserves advice that is honest, specific and easy to understand.

SuccessVisa is an Australian migration and education consultancy established in 2020. We help students, skilled workers, families and employers with study, migration and settlement in Australia, and our services are delivered by MARA registered migration agents together with experienced education and support staff.

We work with two groups of people. Some are planning from India or Fiji, often students and professionals choosing a course, a college or a visa pathway. Others already live in Australia and need advice on permanent residence, a partner or family visa, employer sponsorship or a problem with a visa. In both cases we start the same way, by listening to your situation and telling you what is realistic.

Because visa rules change often, we keep our guidance up to date, explain the reasons behind our advice and show you the options, including the ones we think will not work.

Trust and Compliance

MARA Registered Migration Agents: What That Means for You

What our MARA registration means for you.

SuccessVisa works with migration agents registered with the Migration Agents Registration Authority (MARA). Our registration number is MARN 2318228. Only registered migration agents, lawyers and certain exempt people can lawfully give immigration assistance in Australia, and registered agents must follow a Code of Conduct that covers honest, competent and fair dealing with clients.

That is why you will not hear guarantees from us. We can assess your eligibility, prepare your case and lodge it properly, but the decision belongs to the Department of Home Affairs. If you want to check any migration agent, including us, you can verify their registration on the official register.

Our website content is general information and is not a substitute for advice on your own situation. Visa rules, charges and processing times change, so we date and review our guides.

About SuccessVisa: FAQs

Is SuccessVisa a registered migration agency?

Yes. SuccessVisa is an Australian migration and education consultancy that works with MARA registered migration agents. Our registration number is MARN 2318228. Registered agents must follow the Code of Conduct, which includes giving honest, competent advice.

When was SuccessVisa established?

SuccessVisa was established in 2020 and provides migration and education services for people who want to study, migrate and settle in Australia.

Where are your offices?

We have offices in Parramatta and Toongabbie in New South Wales, Melbourne CBD and Werribee in Victoria, Chandigarh in India and a new office in Suva, Fiji. Addresses, phone numbers and opening hours are listed on this page.

Which services does SuccessVisa offer?

Our migration services include skilled visas (189, 190, 491), employer sponsored visas (482, 186, 494), partner and family visas, visitor visas and protection visas. Our education services include Student visa (500) support, admissions and counselling, Professional Year and RPL assistance. We also help with health insurance, including OSHC, and NAATI.

Do you guarantee a visa outcome?

No. No migration agent can guarantee a visa outcome, because decisions are made by the Australian Government. We give honest advice about your chances, prepare your application carefully and tell you early if a pathway is unlikely to work.

Can I get help from India, Australia or Fiji?

Yes. We have offices in all three countries. We help students and professionals in India who are planning to study or migrate, and people already living in Australia who need advice on permanent residence, partner or family visas, employer sponsorship or a visa problem.

How do I book a consultation?

Use the Book Appointment button on this page or contact your nearest office. We will ask about your goals, your visa history and your timeline, and then explain your realistic options.
