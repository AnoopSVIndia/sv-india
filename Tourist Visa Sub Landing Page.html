Tourist Stream (Subclass 600): Your Complete Guide to Visiting Australia

The tourist stream is the pathway most travellers use to holiday in Australia, see friends and family, or take a cruise, without needing a formal sponsor. This page sets out exactly what a case officer looks for, so you can put together a stronger file the first time.

Who It Suits

Is the Tourist Stream Right for You?

The tourist stream covers most reasons people travel to Australia outside formal sponsorship or business purposes.

  • A holiday, sightseeing, or general leisure travel.
  • Visiting friends or family informally, without them acting as your formal sponsor.
  • Taking a cruise that departs from or calls into Australia.
  • Attending a wedding, family event, or short personal visit.
  • Short, informal study or training of up to three months, where study is not your main purpose.
Eligibility

What a Case Officer Actually Checks

Every tourist stream application is assessed against the genuine visitor criterion, alongside health, character and financial checks.

  • Clear evidence of a genuine intention to visit temporarily and return home once your trip ends.
  • Enough personal funds to cover your own flights, accommodation and daily costs for the whole trip.
  • Ties to your home country, such as steady employment, property, or family, that support your intention to return.
  • A believable and consistent travel purpose that matches your supporting documents.
  • Health requirements met, which can include medical checks for longer stays or certain countries.
  • No substantial criminal record and no unresolved debts to the Australian Government.
  • Travel and health insurance arranged for the length of your stay, since you are responsible for your own medical costs.
Get Organised

Document Checklist for the Tourist Stream

Not every applicant needs every document below, but preparing them upfront makes your file stronger.

  • A valid passport, ideally with several months to spare beyond your intended stay.
  • Recent bank statements or payslips showing you can cover the trip yourself.
  • A clear travel itinerary, including return or onward flights where available.
  • Evidence of employment, study, or business ties at home, such as an employment letter or leave approval.
  • A short cover letter explaining the purpose and length of your visit in your own words.
  • Details of your travel insurance policy for the trip.
  • If visiting family or friends informally, a short letter from them describing the visit can help, even without formal sponsorship.

Timing

How Long Does the Tourist Stream Take?

There is no fixed or guaranteed processing time for any visitor visa.

A complete, well organised tourist stream application with clear evidence tends to move faster than one where the Department has to request more information. Seasonal demand, such as school holidays, can also lengthen queues. Rather than quote a number here that could be out of date, we check the Department's current published ranges for your country and stream before advising you, and you can do the same through the official Home Affairs processing times tool.

Common Questions

Tourist Stream FAQs

Can I include my children on the same application?

No. Every traveller, including children listed in your passport, needs their own separate tourist stream application.

Can I study while I am on a tourist stream visa?

Short, informal study or training of up to three months is generally allowed, but this stream is not designed for a full course of study. If study is your real purpose, a student visa is usually the correct pathway.

Can I change to a student or other visa once I arrive?

Not to a student visa: since 1 July 2024, visitor visa holders cannot apply for a student visa while in Australia. For other visas, it depends on the conditions attached to your specific visa, including whether Condition 8503, No Further Stay, applies. Always check your own grant notice, since this varies by applicant.

Do I need to show a return ticket?

A confirmed return or onward ticket is not always mandatory, but it is strong evidence of a genuine, temporary visit and is worth including if you have one.

Can I visit more than one person or city during my trip?

Yes. The tourist stream is not tied to a single itinerary, though a clear overall travel plan still helps demonstrate a genuine visit.
