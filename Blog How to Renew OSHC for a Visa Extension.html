How to Renew OSHC for a Visa Extension

Quick Answer: To renew OSHC, contact your insurer or use its online extension form before your current cover ends. Buy cover for your whole new visa period before you apply for the visa extension. Never leave a gap in cover.

Your student visa is ending, or you are about to extend it. Now you need to renew OSHC. It can feel confusing, because OSHC does not renew by itself.

This guide shows you when to renew, the exact steps, what you need and what it costs. It also covers gaps, switching insurers and refunds.

What Does It Mean to Renew OSHC?

OSHC and Your Student Visa

Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) is health insurance that student visa holders must have. Your cover must last for your whole stay on a student visa. It must also include any family members who come with you.

This rule is part of your visa conditions. If your visa gets longer, your cover must get longer too.

Does OSHC Renew Automatically?

No. A new Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) does not extend your cover. Deakin University tells students to contact their OSHC provider to renew.

So you need to act. You can top up with your current insurer or buy a new policy from another one. Both options work if there is no gap.

When Should You Renew OSHC?

Before You Apply for the Visa Extension

Buy your extra cover before you lodge your visa application. Allianz Care says this clearly. After you buy, you get a certificate of insurance to include with your application.

The cover must run for the full length of your new visa. The visa end date cannot be changed once it is granted.

How Long Should the Extra Cover Last?

Deakin advises at least 2 months beyond your CoE end date. That covers the time after your course ends, such as results release.

Some courses end at the close of the academic year. In that case, the visa may run a few months past your last class. Your cover must reach that date.

A Simple Timeline

  1. Request a new CoE from your education provider.
  2. Check your policy end date and your new visa dates.
  3. Renew or buy OSHC for the full new period.
  4. Save the new policy certificate.
  5. Lodge your visa application. Deakin suggests applying at least 6 to 8 weeks before your current visa expires.

How to Renew OSHC Step by Step

Step 1: Gather Your Details

Have these ready before you start:

  • Your passport details.
  • Your new Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE).
  • Your current policy certificate and membership number or student ID.
  • Your visa end date.

Medibank's extension form asks for all of these. Other insurers ask for similar details.

Step 2: Choose to Top Up or Switch

You can top up with your current insurer. Or you can buy a new policy from a different one. Either way, the new cover must start the day the old cover ends.

Topping up is usually simpler. Switching can suit you if you find a better price.

Step 3: Extend Online or Contact Your Insurer

Most insurers let you extend online. Here is what the pages we checked say:

  • Allianz Care: Buy a policy for the period you need online or in the Allianz MyHealth app.
  • Medibank: Use the online extension form. If you are in Australia, you must activate your membership first. Payments do not work on Safari, so use Chrome or Firefox.
  • Bupa: Contact Bupa on 1800 888 942 (within Australia, Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm AEDT). Bupa says it may also contact you to review your details for visa needs.
  • nib: Use the "Extend your OSHC" section on its overseas students page, or ask for a quote.

If you are outside Australia, Medibank says to contact the agent or school that arranged your cover.

Step 4: Pay and Save Your Certificate

Check the price before you pay. Then keep your new certificate of insurance. You will need it for your visa application.

Also confirm in writing that your cover starts on the right date.

How Much Does OSHC Cost for 1 Year?

Typical Prices for One Person

As of May 2026, Savvy lists average yearly prices for single hospital-only OSHC between $674 and $719 across five providers. Your price will differ. Always get a live quote.

You pay the full premium upfront for your whole visa period. A longer visa means a larger payment.

What Changes the Price

Three things move the price most:

  • The insurer you pick.
  • Who is covered: one person, a couple or a family. Couples and families cost more.
  • How long you need cover.

Extras, such as dental or optical, add to the cost. They are optional.

Gaps, Switching and Refunds

What Happens If Your OSHC Lapses?

A gap in cover is your responsibility. The Commonwealth Ombudsman warns that you must buy cover for any gap. Your visa will not be granted until the gap is covered.

Allianz Care adds that you cannot claim for treatment you had while uninsured. To extend for visa purposes, you must pay for that uninsured period.

Can You Switch Insurers When You Renew?

Yes, as long as there is no gap. Nib says it recognises waiting periods you have already served on an equivalent cover. Ask your new insurer to confirm this in writing.

If you switch, tell your old insurer in writing. Then ask for a pro-rata refund of the unused cover.

Can You Get a Refund?

Sometimes. Allianz Care lists cases such as a visa extension that Home Affairs did not approve, or leaving Australia early. Refunds are worked out monthly, pro rata, with a minimum of 30 days.

Keep proof, such as a refusal notice or travel record. Rules differ by insurer, so check yours.

What If You Move to a Graduate Visa?

The rules change after your studies. Savvy says that if you move to a graduate or work visa, you switch from OSHC to Overseas Visitors Health Cover (OVHC). Ask your insurer before you cancel.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Waiting until the last day to renew.
  • Buying cover only to your CoE end date, and not beyond it.
  • Assuming a new CoE renews your cover.
  • Forgetting to extend cover for family members.
  • Applying for the visa before you buy the new cover.

Renew OSHC FAQs

How Can I Extend My OSHC?

Contact your insurer or use its online extension form. Buy cover for your full new visa period before you apply for the visa extension. Do this before your current cover ends.

How Much Is OSHC for 1 Year?

As of May 2026, Savvy lists average yearly prices for single hospital-only OSHC from $674 to $719. Couples and families pay more. Get a live quote for your own dates.

How Do I Renew My Allianz Insurance Policy?

Allianz Care says to buy a policy for the period you need, online or in the Allianz MyHealth app. Do it before you apply for a new visa. You then receive a certificate of insurance for your application.

How Do I Renew My Student Visa in Australia?

The visa is a separate step from OSHC. Deakin University lists these: get a new CoE, renew your OSHC, prepare your documents, then apply online to the Department of Home Affairs. Check the official Home Affairs page for your own case.

Does OSHC Renew Automatically?

No. A new CoE does not extend your cover. You must contact your insurer.

How Early Should I Renew OSHC?

Start as early as you can. One guide suggests 4 to 6 weeks before your policy ends. Remember that Deakin suggests lodging the visa application 6 to 8 weeks before your visa expires.

What Happens If My OSHC Runs Out?

You have a gap in cover. You cannot claim for treatment during that time. You may also need to pay for the uninsured period to extend for visa purposes.

Can I Get a Refund If My Visa Extension Is Refused?

Often yes, but it depends on your insurer. Allianz Care lists a visa extension that Home Affairs did not approve as a refund case. Keep your refusal notice.

Do My Family Members Need OSHC Too?

Yes. Family members who come with you on a student visa must also hold OSHC for their stay. Extend their cover at the same time as yours.

In Short

OSHC does not renew by itself. Buy cover for your whole new visa period before you apply for the visa extension. Leave no gap, and keep your certificate.

Start early, check your dates, and confirm everything in writing. If your case is complex, such as a family on one visa or a refused extension, get advice before you pay.
