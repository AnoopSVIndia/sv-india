Getting Organised

What We Confirm Before We Lodge

A final checklist, run through before your application is submitted through ImmiAccount.

  • Confirmation of Enrolment matches the course you were counselled on
  • Genuine Student responses are complete and consistent
  • Financial evidence meets the current AUD 29,710 benchmark, plus dependants
  • OSHC certificate covers your full intended visa period
  • Passport, photos and identity documents are current and correctly formatted
  • English test results or exemption evidence are attached
  • Health examinations and police clearances are complete, where required
  • The correct visa application charge is ready to be paid at lodgement

Questions We Hear Often

Visa Application & Lodgement FAQs

Common questions about preparing and lodging a Student Visa (Subclass 500) application.

Do you lodge the application on my behalf?

Yes. Once your evidence is complete, we prepare and lodge your Student Visa (Subclass 500) application through ImmiAccount on your behalf as your registered migration agent.

What is ImmiAccount?

ImmiAccount is the Department of Home Affairs' online system for lodging and managing Australian visa applications. Your application, documents and correspondence with the Department are all managed through it.

How much is the student visa application charge?

The base charge for the main applicant is AUD 2,500 for applications lodged from 1 July 2026 (AUD 2,050 for ELICOS-only applications), with additional charges for any partner or dependants included, and it is reviewed by the Department from time to time. We confirm the exact current amount before you pay.

What happens if the Department asks for more documents after I lodge?

We respond within the timeframe given, whether that means providing a clarifying document, an updated financial statement, or further information about your Genuine Student circumstances.

Can I track my own application after you lodge it?

Yes, through your ImmiAccount login, though we also track it on your behalf and flag any updates or requests so nothing is missed.

What if my application is refused?

We review the refusal reasons with you and explain what options may be available, which can include a new application or, in limited cases, a review process, depending on your specific circumstances.
