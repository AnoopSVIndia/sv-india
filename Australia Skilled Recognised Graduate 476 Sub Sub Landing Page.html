Skilled Recognised Graduate Visa (Subclass 476): Closed Since 1 July 2024, and What to Do Now

The Skilled Recognised Graduate visa (subclass 476) once let recent engineering graduates from recognised overseas universities live and work in Australia for up to 18 months. It no longer accepts applications. This guide explains what the 476 was, what current holders should do, and which visas engineering graduates can use today.

Is the Skilled Recognised Graduate Visa (476) Still Available?

Short answer: no. The subclass 476 was permanently closed to new applicants from 1 July 2024.

The Australian Government closed the Skilled Recognised Graduate visa to all new applicants, including family members, from 1 July 2024. Home Affairs will not grant any new 476 visas. The change was part of the government's Migration Strategy, which aimed to remove visas that no longer matched its priorities and to simplify the system. The government's explanatory statement notes that the visa was mostly used by applicants outside Australia, and that overseas-trained engineers have other pathways to work in Australia.

The 476 had been capped for the 2023-24 program year, and the cap was reached in January 2024. Applications that were still undecided were treated as not made, and those applicants could request a refund of the visa application charge. If you have seen older websites that still describe how to apply for the 476, they are out of date.

If You Already Hold One

What to Do If You Already Hold a 476 Visa

Existing visas were not cancelled, but the clock is running.

If you were granted a 476 before it closed, your visa remains valid until its expiry date and the work and study rights continue. The stay is generally up to 18 months from your first entry to Australia. You can check your exact expiry date and conditions using VEVO, the Department of Home Affairs' free online check.

When the visa ends, you must leave Australia unless you have been granted another visa, so the planning needs to start well before the expiry date. Babies born in Australia to a 476 holder are still granted a 476 visa automatically. Family members who did not apply with the main holder may find they cannot join or stay under this visa, though other visa options may be available.

  • Check your expiry date and conditions in VEVO this week.
  • Keep complying with every visa condition and keep adequate health insurance.
  • Tell Home Affairs if your contact details or circumstances change.
  • Start your skills assessment or sponsorship discussions at least a year before expiry.
  • Book advice early, because assessments and nominations can take months.

Engineering Skills

Engineers Australia Skills Assessment: The Step That Still Matters

Whichever route you choose, your engineering qualifications will probably be assessed.

Engineers Australia is the assessing authority for engineering occupations. A positive skills assessment is required for the points-tested 189, 190 and 491 visas, and it also helps show employers and states that your qualifications meet Australian standards. The assessment looks at your degree, how it maps to an engineering occupation, and in many cases your work experience.

Each occupation is linked to an ANZSCO code, and picking the wrong one can waste months and the assessment fee. We help you match your degree and your duties to the right occupation before you apply, and check whether that occupation suits the route and state you are considering.

Avoid These

Common Mistakes Engineering Graduates Make After the 476 Closure

Following Outdated Guides

Many older sites still describe how to apply for the 476. It closed on 1 July 2024, so any agent promising a 476 today should be questioned.

Waiting Until the Visa Expires

Assessments, tests and nominations take months. Start planning at least a year before your current visa ends.

Missing the 485 Window

The 485 must be lodged within six months of finishing your course. A late application is not valid.

Assessing the Wrong Occupation

An engineering assessment for the wrong code can fail or fit poorly with the visa route and wastes the fee.

Ignoring Age Limits

The 485 and the points-tested visas have age limits, so your timeline matters.

Breaking Visa Conditions

If you hold a 476, keep complying with its conditions. A breach can affect later applications.

Common Questions

Subclass 476 Visa FAQs

Is the Skilled Recognised Graduate visa (subclass 476) still open?

No. The subclass 476 was permanently closed to new applications from 1 July 2024. Home Affairs will not grant any new 476 visas, so there is no way to apply for it today.

What happens if I applied for a 476 before it closed?

The 476 was capped for the 2023-24 program year, and the cap was reached in January 2024. Applications still undecided at that point were treated as not made, and Home Affairs invited those applicants to request a refund of the visa application charge.

I already hold a 476 visa. What should I do?

Your visa stays valid until its expiry date, and you can keep working and studying on it. Check your expiry date and conditions in VEVO, keep complying with the conditions, and plan your next visa early, because you must leave Australia when it expires unless you have been granted another visa.

Is there a direct replacement for the 476 visa?

No. Home Affairs did not create a like-for-like replacement. Engineering graduates now look at the Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) after Australian study, the employer-sponsored Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482), or the points-tested 189, 190 and 491 visas.

Do I still need an Engineers Australia skills assessment?

If you want to apply for the 189, 190 or 491 in an engineering occupation, yes. Engineers Australia is the assessing authority for engineering occupations. Employer-sponsored and graduate visas have their own requirements, which we check with you.

Did the 476 lead to permanent residence?

No. It was a temporary visa of up to 18 months that gave you time to live and work in Australia. Permanent residence needed a separate visa, such as the 189, 190, 491-then-191 route or an employer-sponsored permanent visa.

Can an overseas engineering graduate get the 485 instead?

The 485 is for people who have completed eligible study in Australia, so an overseas degree alone does not qualify. It also has an age limit, an English test requirement and a six-month window after your course ends.

Where can I check the official status of the 476?

The Department of Home Affairs lists the 476 among visas that are no longer available. Current holders can check their own visa details and expiry date using VEVO.
