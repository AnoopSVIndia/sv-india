The Basics

What Is the Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485)?

A temporary work visa for graduates of Australian study.

The Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) is for international students who have recently finished an eligible Australian course. It lets you stay in Australia for a set period, work full time in any occupation, and study. Many graduates use it to gain Australian work experience, and some use that time to prepare for a skilled or employer-sponsored visa.

It is a temporary visa. It cannot be extended, it does not give a pathway to permanent residence by itself, and the rules on age, English, study and timing are strict. Because you must apply within a short window after your course ends, the best time to plan is before you finish.

485 Visa Streams and How Long You Can Stay

Your qualification decides your stream, and you cannot change stream after you lodge.

Temporary Graduate visa (485) streams and typical stay
StreamWho it is forTypical stay
Post-Higher Education WorkGraduates of a bachelor's, master's or doctoral degree. Occupation does not matter.2 years for bachelor's and coursework master's, 3 years for research master's and doctoral degrees
Post-Vocational Education WorkGraduates with a vocational qualification related to an occupation on the relevant skilled occupation list. A skills assessment is part of the application.Generally 18 months
Second Post-Higher Education WorkHolders of a first 485 who studied and lived in a designated regional area.1 or 2 additional years, depending on the region

Some passport holders may receive longer stays, for example Hong Kong and British National (Overseas) passport holders, and Indian graduates under the Australia-India trade agreement. The Replacement stream is closed to new applicants. Confirm your category on the Department of Home Affairs website.

Eligibility

Temporary Graduate Visa 485 Eligibility Requirements

The main tests for the most common stream, Post-Higher Education Work.

Main requirements for a 485 visa
RequirementWhat it means
AgeGenerally under 35 when you apply. Higher limits apply to research master's and doctoral graduates and to Hong Kong and British National (Overseas) passport holders in the Post-Higher Education Work stream.
Australian studyYou must have completed a CRICOS-registered course, or courses, of at least 2 academic years (at least 16 calendar months) while holding a Student visa.

The Post-Vocational Education Work stream also needs a skills assessment for an occupation on the relevant list and has its own study rules. We check your exact course, dates and passport before advising.

Timing: The Mistake That Costs Graduates Their 485

Most problems come from dates, not documents.

6 months

Apply Within Six Months

Your application must be lodged within 6 months of completing the qualifying course. Missing this window usually ends your eligibility.

In Australia

Lodge While Onshore

You must be in Australia when you apply, and hold or have recently held a Student visa.

12 months

Keep Your English Test Current

The English result must be recent at application time. Book your test early so it does not expire.

Fit Check

Is the Graduate Visa Right for You?

The 485 Visa May Suit You If

  • You have just finished, or are about to finish, an eligible Australian course.
  • You are within the age limit and can meet the English requirement.
  • You want Australian work experience and full work rights.
  • You plan to move later to a skilled or employer-sponsored visa.

Consider Another Route If

  • You are over the age limit or your course is too short.
  • You have missed the 6-month window.
  • You already hold an employer sponsor or qualify for a points-tested visa.
  • You want to keep studying with a new Student visa.

485 Visa Cost and Processing Time

Charges are set per applicant and change each 1 July.

Temporary Graduate visa (485) base application charges from 1 July 2026 (AUD)
ApplicantPost-Higher and Post-Vocational Education WorkSecond Post-Higher Education Work
Main applicantAUD 5,750AUD 2,265
Additional applicant aged 18 or overAUD 2,875AUD 1,140
Additional applicant under 18AUD 1,450AUD 575

Charges are generally not refunded if an application is refused. Other costs include English tests, health insurance, health examinations, police checks, translations, skills assessments for the vocational stream, and professional fees. Because charges are indexed and can change, confirm the figure for your stream on the Department of Home Affairs pricing page before you lodge.

Processing times vary by stream and over time. Higher education applications are often decided faster than vocational applications, which need a skills assessment. Use the Home Affairs processing times tool for the current estimate rather than relying on a fixed number.

Get Organised

Documents to Prepare for a 485 Visa Application

  • Passport, and passports of any family members applying with you.
  • Your course completion letter and academic transcripts.
  • Evidence of your Student visa history in Australia.
  • Your English test result, taken within the required period.
  • Evidence of adequate health insurance for your whole stay.
  • An Australian Federal Police check and other police certificates if required.
  • For the vocational stream, your skills assessment outcome or application.
  • Relationship and identity documents for any family members included.

Common Questions

Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485) FAQs

What is the Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)?

The Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) is a temporary visa that lets recent international graduates stay in Australia after finishing their studies, and live and work here full time. It has separate streams for higher education graduates, vocational graduates and eligible regional graduates.

How long can I stay on a 485 visa?

In the Post-Higher Education Work stream, bachelor's and coursework master's graduates generally get 2 years, and research master's or doctoral graduates get 3 years. The Post-Vocational Education Work stream generally gives 18 months. Some passport holders, such as Hong Kong and British National (Overseas) passport holders and Indian graduates under a trade agreement, may get longer periods.

What is the age limit for the 485 visa?

Most applicants must be under 35 when they apply. Higher limits apply to research master's and doctoral graduates and to Hong Kong and British National (Overseas) passport holders in the Post-Higher Education Work stream. Check your category before you plan around the age cut-off.

What English score do I need for a 485 visa?

The usual requirement is IELTS 6.5 overall with no band below 5.5, or an equivalent score in an accepted test, taken within the 12 months before you apply. Some passport holders are exempt. Accepted tests and score tables are updated by the Department, so check them before booking a test.

How much does a 485 visa cost?

From 1 July 2026 the base application charge for the Post-Higher Education Work and Post-Vocational Education Work streams is reported at AUD 5,750 for the main applicant, AUD 2,875 for each additional applicant aged 18 or over and AUD 1,450 for each applicant under 18. The Second Post-Higher Education Work stream is lower. Health cover, tests, checks and professional fees are extra, so confirm the figures on the Home Affairs pricing page.

When must I apply for the 485 visa?

You must apply while you are in Australia and within 6 months of completing the course you are relying on. You must also hold, or have held within the previous 6 months, a Student visa. Lodging late is a common and avoidable reason for ineligibility.

Can I work on a 485 visa?

Yes. The 485 visa generally allows full-time work in any occupation, and you can also study. The Post-Vocational Education Work stream additionally requires a skills assessment in an occupation on the relevant skilled occupation list as part of the application.

What health insurance do I need for a 485 visa?

You need adequate health insurance for the whole time you are in Australia on the visa. Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) is not accepted for this purpose. Most graduates use Overseas Visitor Health Cover (OVHC) or another policy that meets the requirement, so check before you buy.
