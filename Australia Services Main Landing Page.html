Common Questions

Migration Services FAQs

What migration services does SuccessVisa offer?

SuccessVisa helps with skilled visas (189, 190, 491 and the permanent 191), Recognition of Prior Learning, partner, family and carer visas, student, employer-sponsored and training visas, visitor and business visa options, and Overseas Student Health Cover. Each service has its own guide on this website.

How do I know which Australian visa is right for me?

It depends on your goal, age, skills, English, work history, relationships and where you are now. Use the table on this page to find a starting point, then book an appointment so a registered migration agent can check your options against the current rules.

Are SuccessVisa migration agents registered?

Yes. SuccessVisa works with migration agents registered with the Migration Agents Registration Authority. Our registration number is MARN 2318228. Registered agents must follow the Migration Agents Code of Conduct.

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No. The Department of Home Affairs decides every application, and no registered agent can guarantee an outcome. We assess your situation honestly, prepare a careful application and tell you early if a pathway is unlikely to work.

Can I get help from India, Australia or Fiji?

Yes. We have offices in Parramatta, Toongabbie, Melbourne CBD and Werribee in Australia, Chandigarh in India and Suva in Fiji, and we also meet clients by phone or video.

Are visa charges and rules on these pages up to date?

We review each guide and show the date it was last updated. Visa charges usually change each 1 July and rules can change at any time, so always confirm current figures on the Department of Home Affairs website before you lodge or pay.
