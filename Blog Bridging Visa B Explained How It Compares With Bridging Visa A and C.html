Bridging Visa B Explained: How It Compares with Bridging Visa A and C

Quick Answer: Bridging Visa B (subclass 020) lets you leave Australia and come back while Home Affairs decides your main visa application. You must already hold a Bridging Visa A or C. You apply before you travel, and the fee is about A$575. If you leave without it, you may not be able to return.

Waiting for a visa decision can take months. Life does not pause in that time. A parent may fall ill overseas. A wedding may be coming up. A work trip may be due.

That is where Bridging Visa B helps. It is a travel permission, not a new way to stay.

This guide explains it in plain words. You will see how it differs from Bridging Visa A and C. You will also learn how to apply, the travel rules, the cost and the latest changes from 1 and 30 November 2026.

What Is Bridging Visa B?

Bridging Visa B is a short-term visa with a travel facility. Its subclass number is 020. Home Affairs describes it as a visa that lets you leave and return to Australia once while your main visa is processed.

It does not replace your first bridging visa. It sits on top of it. You keep your Bridging Visa A or C, and the BVB adds the right to travel.

What Bridging Visa B Is For

People often ask, "What is bridging visa B for?" The answer is simple. It is for travel while you wait.

Here are common reasons:

  • A sick or dying family member overseas.
  • A funeral or wedding.
  • A work or business trip.
  • A pressing personal matter that cannot wait.

Home Affairs decides each request on its merits. A strong, genuine reason helps.

Who Can Apply for Bridging Visa B

You can usually apply if you meet these points:

  • You are in Australia.
  • You hold a Bridging Visa A or C.
  • You have a pending application for a main (substantive) visa.
  • You have a good reason to travel.
  • You apply and pay before you leave.

Bridging Visa E holders are treated differently, so check your own case first.

What Bridging Visa B Does Not Do

A BVB does not guarantee your return. It does not make a refused visa safe. It also does not remove a work or study condition on your bridging visa.

Think of it as a pass for one trip. Nothing more.

Bridging Visa A vs B in One Line

A BVA keeps you in Australia. A BVB lets you step out and come back once. You cannot hold a BVB without a BVA or BVC first.

Why Leaving Before You Hold a BVB Is Risky

Many people lose their right to return this way. They leave on a plain BVA or BVC. The visa then ends the moment they depart.

After that, you may need a new visa to come back. That can cost you your whole application.

So the rule is simple. Get the BVB granted first. Then book the flight.

How to Apply for Bridging Visa B

You apply online through ImmiAccount or with the Home Affairs form for Bridging Visa B (Form 1006). Home Affairs says you must pay the fee in ImmiAccount before you submit.

The steps are short. The hard part is the evidence.

Step-by-Step Application Process

  1. Check you hold a BVA or BVC and that your main visa application is still pending.
  2. Choose your travel dates. Keep them realistic and as short as you can.
  3. Log in to ImmiAccount and start the Bridging Visa B application.
  4. Explain why you must travel. Be clear and honest.
  5. Upload your evidence (see the list below).
  6. Pay the fee before you submit.
  7. Submit and wait for the grant. Do not book non-refundable flights until it arrives.

Evidence That Supports Your Travel Reason

Good evidence shows that the trip is genuine and urgent. Gather items that match your reason:

  • A medical letter or hospital report for a sick relative.
  • A death certificate or funeral notice.
  • A wedding invitation with dates.
  • A work letter that explains the business trip.
  • Proof you will return, such as a job, a lease or study plans.
  • A copy of your passport and current bridging visa.

Keep every document clear and recent.

When to Apply

It is best to apply about two weeks to three months before you travel. Applying too late is a common problem. Processing time is not fixed, so give yourself room.

If your trip is an emergency, say so clearly in your application and attach proof.

A Simple Example of a Travel Reason

Write it plainly. For example: "My father is in hospital in Delhi after a heart attack. I need to travel for 3 weeks. My partner and my job are in Sydney, and I will return." Then attach the hospital letter.

Short, honest and backed by proof works best.

Bridging Visa B Travel Rules

The travel rules are where most people slip. Read this section twice before you book a flight.

How Long Can You Travel on a Bridging Visa B?

People often ask, "How long does a bridging visa B last?" The answer depends on your case.

For some skilled and business visa applicants, the travel period can be up to 12 months. For most others, Home Affairs sets the period case by case. It usually matches the reason you gave.

The travel period is fixed. It cannot be extended once it is granted.

The One-Trip Rule

Home Affairs describes a BVB as permission to leave and return once. Plan for a single round trip unless your grant letter says otherwise.

What Happens If You Stay Away Too Long?

If your BVB runs out while you are overseas, it ends. You then have no visa to re-enter Australia. That can put your main application at risk.

Return before the travel period ends. Aim for a few days of buffer.

What Happens If Your Main Visa Is Refused While You Are Away?

The BVB does not last forever after a refusal. It generally ends about 35 days after the refusal of your main visa if you are overseas. Check your grant letter for the exact date.

This is why travel carries real risk.

Does Travel Reset Your Time in Australia?

No. A short trip on a BVB does not reset section 48, the rule that blocks some people from applying for another visa onshore. Your history stays with you.

Latest Changes from November 2026

Two changes matter right now:

  • From 1 November 2026: new limits apply to Protection visa applicants who want to travel. If you applied for a Protection visa, check with a MARA agent before you leave.
  • From 30 November 2026: shorter travel periods are expected for people waiting on employer-sponsored visas.

Confirm the latest details on the Home Affairs website.

Work and Study Conditions

A BVB does not remove a work or study condition. Your conditions stay the same as on your first bridging visa. If you need work rights, ask for them on that visa, not on the BVB.

Immigration Clearance Is Not Guaranteed

A BVB is a discretionary visa. Border officers still check you on return. Carry your visa grant, passport and proof of your reason for travel.

Bridging Visa B Cost and Processing Time

Money and time are the two big worries. Here is what is known as of 26 November 2026.

How Much Does Bridging Visa B Cost?

The Bridging Visa B application charge is A$575. Passport holders from certain Pacific countries pay a reduced A$195.

Always check the current fee in ImmiAccount or on the Home Affairs website before you pay.

Other Costs to Plan For

The visa fee is only part of the bill. Flights, travel insurance and time off work add up fast.

Also keep in mind that a bridging visa fee is generally not refunded if you change your plans. Ask before you pay if your trip is uncertain.

How Long Does Bridging Visa B Take?

Home Affairs does not publish a set processing time for Bridging Visa B. Some cases move in days. Others take weeks.

So plan early. Lodge at least a few weeks before your flight, and earlier if you can.

What Slows a Decision Down

  • Missing or unclear evidence.
  • A travel reason that does not look genuine.
  • Trouble with your main visa, such as a pending health or character check.
  • Busy periods, such as before holidays.

Can You Pay Less?

There is no discount for urgency. The only reduced charge is for the Pacific passport holders mentioned above. Do not pay anyone who offers a "cheaper" BVB. Pay only through ImmiAccount.

Can a Bridging Visa B Be Refused?

Yes. A BVB is discretionary. Home Affairs can say no, even if you meet the basic rules.

Why Bridging Visa B Applications Get Refused

  • The reason for travel is weak or not proven.
  • The trip is too long for the reason given.
  • You do not hold a BVA or BVC.
  • Your main application is no longer pending.
  • Your records show serious past breaches.

A refusal of the BVB does not cancel your first bridging visa. You stay on it. You just cannot travel safely.

Is It Hard to Get a Bridging Visa B?

It is not hard if your reason is real and your papers are in order. It gets hard when evidence is thin or the trip looks like a holiday.

Home Affairs does not publish approval rates, so no one can give you a true percentage. Be wary of anyone who does.

What to Do If Your Application Is Refused

Stay in Australia. Do not travel. Read the refusal reasons, then fix the gaps and apply again with better proof.

You can also ask a MARA agent or lawyer about review options, including judicial review. The Administrative Review Tribunal (ART) does not review every bridging visa decision, so get advice first.

Common Bridging Visa B Mistakes to Avoid

Most problems come from rushing. Avoid these:

  • Leaving before the grant. Your BVA or BVC may end the moment you depart.
  • Booking flights first. Wait for the visa, or choose refundable fares.
  • Staying past the travel period. The BVB ends, and re-entry becomes hard.
  • Giving a vague reason. "Family matters" is not enough. Be specific.
  • Skipping proof. Claims without documents carry little weight.
  • Ignoring the new November 2026 rules. They affect Protection and employer-sponsored applicants.
  • Assuming it works like a tourist visa. It is a one-trip permission with limits.
  • Paying through an unofficial link. Use ImmiAccount only.

Frequently Asked Questions About Bridging Visa B

How Long Does a Bridging Visa B Last?

It lasts for the travel period Home Affairs grants you. For some skilled and business visa applicants this can be up to 12 months. For most people it is shorter and matches the reason for travel. It cannot be extended, and it ends if it expires while you are overseas.

Is It Hard to Get a Bridging Visa B?

Not if your reason is genuine and well proven. It becomes hard when the evidence is weak or the trip looks like leisure. Home Affairs publishes no approval rate, so treat any percentage you see with care.

Can a Bridging Visa B Be Refused?

Yes. It is a discretionary visa. Common reasons are a weak travel reason, missing evidence, or not holding a Bridging Visa A or C. A refusal does not cancel your first bridging visa.

What Is Bridging Visa B For?

It lets you leave Australia and return once while your main visa application is being processed. People use it for family emergencies, weddings, funerals and business trips.

How Much Does Bridging Visa B Cost?

The charge is about A$575, or A$195 for some Pacific passport holders. Confirm the amount in ImmiAccount before you pay.

Can I Travel on a Bridging Visa A?

Not safely. A BVA does not carry travel rights. If you leave on it, the visa may end. Get a BVB granted first.

Can I Apply for a Bridging Visa B from Overseas?

In general, no. You apply while you are in Australia, before you leave. If you are already overseas without a BVB, you may need a new visa to return.

Can I Take More than One Trip on a Bridging Visa B?

Home Affairs describes it as leave and return once. Plan for one trip unless your grant says otherwise.

Can I Work on a Bridging Visa B?

Your work rights stay the same as on your first bridging visa. The BVB does not add or remove them.

Does a Bridging Visa B Guarantee I Can Return to Australia?

No. You still go through border checks on return. Keep your grant letter, passport and proof of your travel reason with you.

In Short

Bridging Visa B gives you one trip out of Australia and back while your main visa is processed. You need a Bridging Visa A or C first, a strong reason, good proof and about A$575. Never leave before it is granted, and always return before it ends.

Plan early, keep your plans flexible and check the November 2026 changes. If your case is complex, speak to a MARA-registered agent before you book a flight.
