The Basics

What Overseas Student Health Cover Actually Covers

OSHC is health insurance designed specifically for international students, structured to meet visa condition 8501 rather than general private health insurance rules. Basic OSHC typically covers doctor visits, hospital treatment, emergency ambulance transport and a limited amount of prescription medicine, with dental, optical and physiotherapy generally not covered; only some insurers, such as Bupa, offer an optional extras add-on.

Mandatory for Your Full Visa

OSHC must run for as long as you hold a Subclass 500 visa, starting on or before your arrival, with no gaps allowed.

Five Approved Insurers

Bupa, Medibank, ahm (a Medibank brand), nib and Allianz Care are currently the government-approved OSHC providers; any policy outside these does not satisfy your visa.

Paid Upfront, In Full

OSHC premiums are paid for your full period of cover before your visa is lodged, not month to month.

Basic vs Extras

Basic OSHC covers hospital, medical and ambulance costs; dental, optical and physiotherapy are not part of standard OSHC, and only some insurers, such as Bupa, offer an extras add-on.

Questions We Hear Often

OSHC Health Cover FAQs

Common questions about Overseas Student Health Cover for the Australia student visa.

Is OSHC compulsory for a Student Visa (Subclass 500)?

Yes. OSHC is a visa condition (condition 8501) for as long as you hold a Subclass 500 visa, and it must run for your full visa period, not only your course dates.

Which OSHC providers are approved?

Bupa, Medibank, ahm, nib and Allianz Care (Peoplecare) are currently the government-approved insurers for OSHC. A policy from an insurer outside this list does not satisfy your visa condition.

Do I have to use the OSHC provider my university recommends?

No. Many providers have a preferred OSHC partner, but you are free to choose any of the five approved insurers, provided the cover meets your visa's requirements.

Does OSHC cover dental and optical treatment?

Basic OSHC generally does not. Dental, optical and physiotherapy are only covered if your insurer offers an extras add-on, such as Bupa's, and you buy it on top of your basic policy.

Can I pay for OSHC monthly instead of upfront?

OSHC premiums are generally required to be paid in full for your total period of cover before your visa is lodged, even if a provider quotes a monthly figure.

What happens if there is a gap in my OSHC cover?

A gap in OSHC cover can put you in breach of visa condition 8501. We check your dates carefully so cover starts on or before arrival and runs without interruption to your visa expiry.
